## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

Use the three hedges when a source does not close a claim: `not established by these sources` / `the public pages cannot determine` / `this specific connection is ruled out`.

- Public layer of Anpu-Deed-1983-047 proves the exhibit catalog wording. It does not prove who stood at the burial, and it does not prove every character on the brick was read.
- Internal memo is staff sorting. It is not a relocation approval, and it does not prove the back wall was demolished.
- Restricted transcription proves the locked slip was copied that way. It is not a unique authentic deed, and it is not a warrant to open a grave.
- House-plot notice (1991) governs a lived-in house. It does not govern yin-house title.
- Cemetery use-certificate governs a West Slope sector. It does not name the Hou house, the stone steps, or the back wall.
- Ganzhi (`jiazi`) may be formula. The note must not convert it into a Gregorian date.
- Dongxi Cen Shoushan (1962) and Nanling Cen Shoushan (1983 winter month) are not automatically one person. Village and year must be checked. `this specific connection is ruled out` applies only to treating the Dongxi gazetteer line as the 047 deceased.
- Hou Qiming’s classified ad proves which east bound he fears. It does not identify who changed the internal line.
- Ge Qiuping’s board post is a side thread. It does not block submit and does not prove Ge Wanchuan paid for the plot.
- Scanner dust, the lame-sheep thread, and the shallow-canal brief produce no 047 field.
- Anpu-Deed-1979-012 is another household. Its fields must not enter the 047 note.
- Missing proof is not innocence. A locked cabinet is not exoneration.

Do not teach forging, carving, rubbing, or rewriting a maidiquan. FAQ and encyclopedia already refuse templates, cinnabar recipes, and “write one in this format.” Keep that refusal.
