1 Location

Nanling sits southwest of the county seat. West Slope faces the ditch. East Ridge is on the other side of the ridge. Two Nanling deeds in a maidiquan catalog are often read by outsiders as one plot. Local people do not say stone steps and ridge beam as one place-name.

2 Cemetery and households

In 1991 West Slope was drawn into a cemetery sector. The stone steps stayed a residents' path. Houses still standing go by house plot. Graves inside a sector go by use certificate. When the two certificates do not match, the office marks conflict. The township does not relocate for either side.

3 Clerk's office

The township clerk's office witnessed signatures in the 1980s. Among those later transferred was Shen Jingzhi. This lemma does not bring in a private blog. For how he later talked about cabinets, go to his own page.
