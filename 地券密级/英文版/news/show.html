County archives exhibit Nanling maidiquan

Source: culture news · 2016-09-18

A West Slope Hou-family deed in the case was written as a complete story: qianzhu Hou Wanshi, wangzhe Hou Chengshan, zhongbao Houtu. Director Shen Jingzhi said at the ribbon-cutting that an exhibit should be readable; details stay in the stacks.

A reporter asked whether the original could be photographed. The archive said scans yes, the brick face no. The reporter asked whether another transcription existed. The director said: the one outside the cabinet has to be able to hang.

This report follows the press-event record. It did not check every character on the brick. A reader who only sees this piece would think the Hou deed has one version.
