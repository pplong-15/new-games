Comrade Shen Jingzhi of the county archives bureau retires

Source: personnel brief · 2018-12-02

Shen Jingzhi, Nanling Township. Early years in the township clerk's office, later transferred to the county archives, served through director. Colleagues said he handled the Nanling grave-deed exhibit. He himself said one sentence: lock what a cabinet should lock.

The brief did not write maidiquan, zhongbao, or a split-cabinet instruction. Those words are in the house log. This paper issued personnel only.

After retirement he opened a personal blog. Updates were slow. The link is not on this paper's site. If duty needs to read it, remember the address yourself.
