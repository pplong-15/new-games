Since early summer, Nanling's old canal has run low

Source: society brief · 2024-07-19

The water station said rainfall was light and dredging of the old canal was not yet scheduled. People who keep sheep said animals drink from the canal. There is no plan to compare this with cemetery stake numbers or a house-plot east bound.

A reader wrote to ask whether the old canal was the west bound on a maidiquan. This paper did not check fields with the archives, and it will not write shallow water from a water station as a change of bound. The brief ends here.

If duty sees someone on the township board mixing the canal with sizhi, treat it as talk. This piece cannot be written into any 047 box. Finding the sheep does not change that. It has nothing to do with qianzhu.
