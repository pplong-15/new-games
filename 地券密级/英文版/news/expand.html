Nanling cemetery West Slope expansion broke ground yesterday

Source: this paper's correspondent · 1991-04-07

The township said old graves were scattered and hard to manage. West Slope was drawn into a cemetery sector. The stone-steps strip stays a residents' path and is not folded into the burial area. The report did not name the Hou family. It did not name the Ge family.

Some villagers worried the expansion would move burials not yet relocated. The township clerk then said: stakes first, no bones moved. This paper wrote it that way. Stake numbers later entered the use certificate. Nobody at a meeting checked them against sizhi on the old deeds.

This article can only prove there was an expansion line in 1991. It does not prove whether the 047 grave was staked, and it is not a permit to open a grave.
