Nanling Township house-plot notice extract

1991 · one West Slope household

Office column Cemetery certificate Request

Householder line later handwritten Hou Qiming (succeeding his father’s household). House east to the stone steps, west to the family’s pigsty, south to the township road, north to the lower edge of the ridge spine. The notice governs a house still lived in. It does not govern a grave on the slope.

Hold this east bound against the public-layer maidiquan: stone steps to stone steps. Hold it against the internal layer “Hou house back wall”: they do not match. If the office sees the internal sentence, it will ask about that wall first. It will not ask the deceased’s name first.

The notice is not a yin contract, and it cannot be used to teach anyone to alter a deed. Extract ends here. The original notice is in the township old files. This office has one corner of a photocopy.
