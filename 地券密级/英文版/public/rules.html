Open-viewing notice

Issued: open-review groupApplies to electronic copies

Readers may view scans and cataloging already posted. Brick originals, untranscribed vermilion, and paper in the restricted cabinet are not lent, not photographed, and not copied into a new deed. This archive does not offer a “rewrite one in this format” service.

A viewing note is filled by duty staff. It only records what each layer’s catalog shows, and whether the layers fight. A note is not a certificate. It cannot approve a relocation, and it cannot serve as a house-plot change.

If living descendants dispute sizhi, they file at the Heritage Office. They do not call night shift. Night-shift phones only report whether the machines are still on.

Copying public-layer fields into a personal blog, a township board, or a classified ad is not blocked by this site and is not endorsed. The archive only recognizes the version submitted from the digitization desk.
