The paper on the West Stack door

Door office copied this for the public siteKey is not with night shift

West Stack locks a double bolt on Fridays. Brick deeds, untranscribed vermilion, restricted-cabinet slips — all inside. If a visitor rings, the night-shift assistant may only say: bring a letter of introduction tomorrow. Do not pass a digitization badge as a key.

Some people think seeing a scan means they have been in the West Stack. No. Scans were shot under day-shift lights. Missing corners, glare, cinnabar gone black — all possible. Matching fields on a screen is not the same as touching a brick.

The original slip for 047, per the sort memo, is in the restricted cabinet. This door slip does not write which drawer. Writing it would not open it. The slip reminds one thing: tonight’s work ends on the screen.

The daytime door-office number for stack complaints is posted on that shift. The night extension will ring. Nobody logs it. Do not write an extension into a viewing note. The office will not take it.
