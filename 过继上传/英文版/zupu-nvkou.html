Daughters not entered means the line table does not list daughters born to this branch. That is why the two characters Wanhe do not enter Huai-017. This is an old practice in the client contract. It is not the county hukou rule. Whether the hukou book has a daughter, this company neither checks nor copies onto the table.

People phone and swear we “wiped the girl.” Duty slip: go find the branch head who signed. This company does not invent the rule. It executes article 3 as signed. If the client pays for an extra leaf, an annex may write “so-and-so, married out.” Still not into the main line table.

Bochuan did not buy an extra leaf. On the e-book Wanhe equals a blank. Use the blank to prove she does not live at No. 17: no. Use it to prove she is not a daughter: also no. It only shows: this company, by contract, did not write her into a cell.

Jiantiao, uxorilocal marriage, surname change: the rule also says “this company does not open those cells.” Anyone who treats the rule as a court paper owns the mistake. If the street wants to write a coresidence fact, go to hukou and the grid. Do not come to change our price list.
