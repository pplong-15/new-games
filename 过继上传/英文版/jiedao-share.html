Huaiyin Subdistrict leftover note
Night desk Intake notice

This desk does not award property

In an xiupu year people often bind “has an heir” to a leftover back-yard room and come to the grid to have it settled. The street does not endorse that bind. Leftovers, empty ground under an eave, that back-yard room: if there is a fight, this post only uploads a dispute class. It does not award who owns. It does not measure walls for a branch head. It does not confirm a male line for the genealogy office.

That back-yard room at No. 17 has shown up on the ticket under three names: watched as heir property, leftover room, share. Different names. This desk treats all of them as “someone is fighting over a room.” Whether a share mediator is sent depends on the class already locked. It does not depend on Bochuan’s tape.

“Empty ground” on the Liutiao Lane old ticket is drip, not this household’s back yard. Eave length does not carry over. If this note overlaps the resident letter’s “don’t empty the house,” the letter is still the statement. This note only sets the right: we do not award property.

People ask to write the child’s name onto a share table before mediation. Rights table has no such row. Class first. Then dispatch. School roll, bedding: not in this note’s intake.
