Tan Xiuzhi, next door to No. 17. Someone told me to write it for the street, so I write. I did not go into the mourning hall. Do not take my words as a statement.

After Shouren went, at night No. 17 is still Wanhe who opens. Kitchen light first. That Xiaoman boy came for the funeral, came again the photo day, stood at the table writing, pencil backward. Never saw him haul bedding in.

Bochuan comes every day to measure that back-yard room. Tape snaps. He swears at the grit. He talks to Shouli. I hear it through the wall. Share. Not which bed the child moves to. The day Suqiu stamped, I watched through the door crack. Her hand shook. Bochuan grabbed her finger and pressed it down. She did not nod. Don’t ask me what the red paper said. I can’t read all the characters.

If you come to the door, come in daylight. A car stops, dogs in the lane go, and I have to come out and explain.
