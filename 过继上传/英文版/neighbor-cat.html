Lost & found User’s own words

Du Guihua, Quyuan Road. Morning of the 14th, half-grown yellow cat gone. Red cloth on the neck still from New Year. It does not go into Zaoshu Lane. Sleeps in the carton at No. 9’s door. Somebody don’t take a cat post for No. 17’s business.

That afternoon a white one stopped at the lane mouth. Words on the door, I didn’t read them. Thought they were catching cats. I ran out swearing. Driver said they were asking at a door, not catching cats. Which house I didn’t catch. Anyway not here for Huangguo. Huangguo is the cat’s name. Don’t laugh.

Night it crawled out of the trash room itself, chicken fluff on the mouth. Found. White car came once more later, parked farther, dogs went a while. I only hate door cars narrowing the lane mouth so a bike has to tilt. Whose ticket I don’t care. Cat business ends here.

Admin if you move this post into the Zaoshu section, look at the address first. 9 Quyuan Road and No. 17 have a canal between them. Seven minutes on foot. Those two characters “No. 9” don’t stick onto somebody else’s ticket.
