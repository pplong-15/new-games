Municipal No. 3 door

That round iron lid at No. 3 Zaoshu Lane’s door, night of the 16th it sounded. I live on the second floor. Like a foot grinding. Or a bike riding the rim. Not No. 17’s back door. No. 17’s back door faces the canal. No. 3 faces the lane heart. Two drying yards in between.

Somebody replied that sound was a tape. I have heard a tape. Teeth come in one section at a time. A manhole is iron on iron, heavy, short ring. Two sounds are not one thing. Don’t write it again as someone measuring a wall at midnight.

Sanitation came the morning of the 17th. Lid loose, two bolts short. Afternoon they welded it. Grind it now, it doesn’t sound. Anyone wants evidence, look at the weld at No. 3’s wall foot. Don’t run to No. 17 and bang the door. This post is the manhole. Not whose lisi.

Admin pinned a line once: municipal stays municipal. I agree. Anyone changes this title to “who opens at night,” I apply to fold it.
