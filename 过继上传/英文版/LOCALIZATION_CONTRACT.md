## Positioning

Working title: **Upload the Heir**.

The edition must read as an English-language night shift *inside* a Chinese subdistrict stability desk. The player is Bian Liang. He solves provenance, identity, chronology, and what a field *means* once a huifang car uses his words — not a Chinese literacy test.

Registers stay split (from `COPY_NOTES.md`):

- **UI / desk / help / toasts**: clean standard English. No mechanic slang.
- **You Guang notes and internal mail**: lazy, clipped, one job — count the fields.
- **Hukou / policy / subsidy / office / history / share memo**: dry window prose, passive where the Chinese is bureaucratic. Dates and codes first. No folklore lecture.
- **Sishu custody pages**: semi-formal oral archive. Incense and heir rules keep command force. No lyricism.
- **Jiangzuo Genealogy**: 2005 company boilerplate. Price per page. “We do not open that cell.”
- **Auntie Tan / alley board**: rambling, “I did not go in,” facts leak from complaint. Noise posts keep their own alleys.
- **Suqiu’s letter**: revision mid-sentence, rural, wants the letter deleted. Not tidy essays.
- **Wanhe / classmates**: lazy fragments. “Here. Don’t forward.” Gate copy differs per locked page.
- **Phone transcript**: machine punctuation. Bochuan says “branch” more than “child.”
- **Bian Liang log**: short, afraid of audit. Heard the tape teeth; will not write “eyewitness.”
- **Result / huifang script**: factual close. No gold-sentence ending. No “not X but Y” rhetoric. Reasons follow the ticked blocks.

## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

- A red sishu, witnesses, and a July genealogy backfill can look like a complete paper trail. That does not establish inbound hukou, and it does not establish that Xiaoman slept at No. 17.
- Hukou scans establish who is on the roll, that inbound is empty since 2023-01, that a 2026-05-20 red-paper “compare” was refused, and that Xiaoman remains on 9 Quyuan Road. They do not prove the red paper is forged.
- Finger ink on the same table as a joss-paper receipt book cannot identify which sheet Suqiu stamped. Custody will not invent “she agreed.”
- “Daughters not entered” is Jiangzuo contract article 3, not a county hukou rule. A blank cell does not prove Wanhe does not live at No. 17, and does not prove she is not a daughter.
- Jiantiao (one son serving two ancestral lines) is oral only. No upload block. Do not invent a form.
- Auntie Tan writes what she heard through a wall. Not a witness statement.
- Cat / water / manhole posts are near-answer noise. They cannot prove who lives at No. 17 or who measured the room.
- Suqiu’s letter is what she says now. It cannot reconstruct every minute of the mourning hall.
- Last year’s care-subsidy refusal (“coresidence not clearly written”) points at “care not on any roll.” It cannot, alone, prove she still lives there now.
- Ticket Zao-083 (Liutiao Lane, Yao drip) has no extract button. Do not carry eave length into this ticket.
- Missing proof is not innocence. Use: `not established by these sources` / `the public pages cannot determine` / `this specific connection is ruled out` when a page already hedges.

## Serious allegations

This game does not write population sale, and does not write “a different surname entering the house is punished.” Xiaoman and Wanhe are both Geng. Cost is litigation, share, and huifang wording — not divine punishment.

Do not upgrade:

- Bochuan pressing Suqiu’s finger → proven forgery or a criminal finding.
- Empty inbound column → Xiaoman “was never meant to be heir.”
- Wanhe paying the bill → a legal heir or a street-approved coresident.
- Clash ending → proof either household lied. It is two uploaded versions the system both accepted.
