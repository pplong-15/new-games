Water cut Nanbu zone

Nanbu water, 2026-08-12, valve service. Slip posted at the grain shop door. Plenty of people on the Zaoshu side never saw it. Somebody changed the title to “whole street cut,” so Auntie Tan came asking if she should store buckets. Not the whole street. Nanbu and Zaoshu are not the same booster pump.

I pack up at Nanbu market. That noon the tap spit yellow rust. Afternoon it was gone. People who had buckets were fine. People who didn’t bought bottled water. Shop jacked the price. Water later replied: planned service, posted a day early, wind blew the slip into the ditch, they don’t cover bottled water.

Somebody downstairs put 17 Zaoshu Lane on the cut too, said lights came on late because nobody washed dishes at night. Don’t borrow a water cut to write another house. Nanbu service does not match who opens a door or who measures a yard. Bucket business stays in Nanbu. Don’t copy it into another section.

Morning of the 13th water came back. Still have to run a pot for the rust. This post ends on the 13th. Anyone still asking “did that household shut the valve on purpose,” go ask water. I only take vegetables.
