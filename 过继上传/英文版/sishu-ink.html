Finger ink — custodian side note

Custody no. Geng-Huaiwen-19 annex 2Found on the copy day

At copy time, ash was caught in the ridges of Suqiu’s index finger. Ink was thin. The stamp is not beside a name. It sits in the blank corner. On the table then: a joss-paper receipt book. Pale ink on the book edge too. This office cannot tell whether she pressed the sishu, or a page from the receipt book that had been folded underneath.

The joss table is on the left of the main room, three steps from the long table. Someone later asked: if she was at the joss table, how did a stamp land on the red paper. The note writes what was seen. It does not write a guess. Director said the hall was a mess, paper travels, hands shake, ash mixes into leftover slab ink.

SeenRecordedThis office does not write

The copy clerk saw ink on the finger and the joss table. What she took the sheet to be, the note cannot restore. If the grid later receives her own account, take her letter. This cabinet does not change her wording for her. Original stitching not opened. No instrument. Authentication box blank.

When Bochuan took the key he said “a stamp on it counts.” Director did not answer. Last line of the note only: which sheet the stamp is on, this cabinet cannot say.
