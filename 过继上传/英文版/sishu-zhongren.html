Witness column — custody note

Custody no. Geng-Huaiwen-19 annex 1Copied lunar May 21

The witness column on the original writes two names only: Geng Bochuan, Geng Shouli. No outside surname. The writer box is also Bochuan. That is: the hand that held the brush is one of the witnesses. This office copies. It does not judge whether such writing fits the old rule.

Shouli’s mark is not in the middle of the long table. The ink drop sits on the stretch by the threshold. A nephew who stood feeding the fire did not sign. Director asked him. He said he only added fire; he cannot read all the characters. The hall was crowded. Who came first, the copy cannot fix.

RoleNameTrace of presence
Writer and witnessGeng BochuanWhole hand matches the original in the cabinet.
Birth-branch witnessGeng ShouliMarked. Did not write “willing” as a second phrase.
Looking onNephew (no name left)Not entered in the column.

Director in pencil: witness and writer stacked as one person. This cabinet copies. It does not admit that “both branches nodded.” Whether Shouli then wanted the child moved, the paper has no second sentence. Whether Suqiu was present: her name is not in the witness column. Finger ink is noted on the next page.

In July the genealogy office came for the original. Key still with Bochuan. This office opened the cabinet and matched the characters once. The two witness cells have no later add. Whether another sheet was written elsewhere after, this cabinet has not seen it.
