Tape again. Measuring that back-yard roomZaoshuAug 19

Tan Xiuzhi, next door to No. 17. Last post, someone messaged asking if I heard wrong. I write it once more. Early August through the 19th, tape almost every day. Snap. Teeth coming in. Snap again. Not the main room. That back-yard room. Depth, he measured it several times.

Once Bochuan brought a mud-and-brick man. Through the wall I heard him: tear out the stove, that room has to be emptied. Suqiu swore through the window, pot still there, who dares. The mason left on his own. Said he doesn’t take that kind of job. I did not go into the yard. That white mark at the wall foot I saw hanging laundry. Like chalk. Or like the tape hook scraping.

When they fight they mention the child. Not much. What they mention more is “need an heir to divide” and “xiupu this year.” Writing it as the kid moving house, that’s your business. I only dare write: the tape measured that one room. Not anybody’s bedroll.

Don’t park the car at my door. Dog goes, I have to go out and explain, and if I explain too much the lane calls me a mouth. This post ends here. No more private messages.
