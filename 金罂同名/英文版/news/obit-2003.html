Comrade LinAsheng dies

County paper, society page. 2003-08-19. Plant publicity desk supplied copy.

County CementPlant retiree Comrade LinAsheng died of illness at the county hospital on 2003-08-12, aged sixty-five. Comrade Lin was born in 1938. In 1996 he moved from PersimmonFlat into a GongrenRoad collective household and worked seven years in the finishing shop. Last address: GongrenRoad 8 tongzilou.

Survivors: wife QiuXiulan, son LinBonian. Funeral kept small. No cash gifts. No roadside altar. The plant sent one wreath. The name was written on the side of the ribbon. No photograph ran.

Same page, one added line: GongrenRoad tongzilou has already been listed for a shed-row survey. Doorplate still there. Stair lights often dead. Death-notice address follows the collective household. Do not write PersimmonFlat 12. Twelve was the village doorplate before MoveOut.
