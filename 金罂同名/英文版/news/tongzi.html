GongrenRoad CementPlant dorm listed for shed-row work

City-build preview. 2008-03. Housing office supplied copy.

GongrenRoad tongzilou listed for a demolition survey. Residents are mostly collective households who entered the plant after 1996. The preview asks for doorplates, not milk names. Two households with the same name must fill birth years separately.

The copy gave one example: finishing-shop Lin household, birth year 1938, spouse Qiu, doorplate No. 8. Chengguan EastLane residents are not on this building's roll. LimeKiln kiln workers are not on this county's resettlement list.

A preview cannot stand as a death notice, still less as a niche certificate. It only locks a dorm address. Which hall a jinying later entered must be matched at the township board and the hall. The paper has no hall-number column.
