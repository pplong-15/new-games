EastLane Mr. LinAsheng dies of illness

Evening paper, community page. 2016-11-06. Neighbors supplied copy.

Chengguan EastLane 3 resident Mr. LinAsheng died of illness on 2016-11-04, aged sixty-five. Mr. Lin was born in 1951. Household stayed on EastLane. He did not move to the plant, and he did not move back to PersimmonFlat. Wife WuWanxiang. Daughter LinQiuhe out of town. Funeral arranged by WuWanxiang.

Morning of the 7th, placed in Jinying Hall SouthHall. No roadside altar. EastLane rain that night. Water not down. Neighbors who walked him out stepped on boards. The short ran no photograph and did not write the three syllables CementPlant.

An editor later added one small line: this paper had another same-name death notice in 2003. Birth year and address both differ. Families claiming a niche should separate them themselves. The small type sat in the gutter. Many people never saw it.
