Same-name check — copy paper

Tape on the underside of the tube. No stamp. FangYongshi's hand. QiuWanqiu never revised it.

1. Name matches. Then birth year. If two death notices make the same age, split them on the birth year.

2. Then registered address. Ancestral house, collective household, lessee doorplate — three columns, not one place.

3. Then MoveOut. A posted name is common when an attachment is short. It does not mean a person is still in the niche.

4. Night shift only recommends. No moving cabinets. No changing the original niche. No signing for a family.

Red-pen note: a neighbor-county LimeKiln kiln worker is also called LinAsheng. Household writes 1944. Jinying not in this hall. Anyone who comes in on the name, send them back to the neighbor county. Tonight's two rows are only WestHall and SouthHall. Do not fill in the kiln line.

Lookup uses the year as printed, four digits, like 1996. This machine wants one continuous token. No spaces.

Tape gone tacky. Corner lifted a finger's width.
