Half a yellow scrap — WestHall 07

Paper clip on the inside back of the handover book. Pencil on the edge: 2011-04, rain.

Old tag remnant

Front still readable: Lin, sheng, West. The middle mark is a blot. Back, one pencil line: pasted again. Cabinet-face type is not the book.

Chu Ci held it to the lamp. The card on the cabinet door now is later print type. Ink newer than the lease book by a length. The bend of water stain on the copy's upper right matches this ragged edge. Rain. Someone pasted again.

A cabinet door is a later mark. The book is the lease ledger. Type pasted on after a soaking cannot decide which niche you claim tonight. A niche short an attachment can still show a posted name. Night shift may only compare copies and the township board. It may not change a hall number from a cabinet door.

FangYongshi left this half-scrap the day he walked. Not so the next shift would treat it as an original. So nobody would only look up at the cabinets.

Tag edge still bites a little rain rust.
