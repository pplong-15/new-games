Four slips

FangYongshi stuffed them in as he left. Finish one before you lift the next. He wrote: do not let Qiuhe see.

First slip

Two identical names. Do not fill a hall number yet. Finish year and address. The people will come apart themselves.

Second slip

Township board has a list. Old papers have death notices. Read each as each. Do not weld them into one life in your head.

Third slip

That mother's name in her mouth is printed on only one of the placement copies. Whichever copy has the name is the one that belongs with her.

Fourth slip

SouthHall matches WuWanxiang. On the WestHall side, Bonian already moved the jinying. Book not cleared. You can recommend SouthHall, or report the same name. Do not claim the WestHall niche tonight.

Lift the next slip

Slip paper yellowed. Staple rusted on one leg.
