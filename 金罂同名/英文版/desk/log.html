Last week — three days pulled

Book is by day. Chu Ci only copied what touches tonight's two rows. The rest of the idle accounts stayed.

08-12. LinBonian called in. Said the WestHall jinying was already moved. Anyone who claims it again answers to him. I told him to supply the cremation stub. He swore once and hung up.

08-13. I copied the call into the handover book. Receipt still missing that one sheet. Book still posts LinAsheng on WestHall. I wanted to clear it. No clear-book stamp in the drawer.

08-14. Walked. Slips in the drawer. Same name, first match the year. SouthHall lessee is WuWanxiang. Do not listen to which village they name first. I already wrote the wet-tag file. Do not keep asking about the cabinet door.

A later page has a neighbor-county inquiry: LimeKiln called, asked if this hall has the 1944 Asheng. I said no. That niche is not in Tongxi.

Log cover fuzzing. One binding thread slack.
