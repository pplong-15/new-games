Incoming — LinBonian

Night of 20 March. FangYongshi's hand. Paper pulled from under the phone.

I copied his tone: after MoveOut, take no more claims. WestHall 07. My father's. Jinying went to the ancestral mountain. Your book still posting him is your missing paper, not me not filing.

Chu Ci later checked the township board. The 2018 filing matches hall code and birth year 1938. What is missing is the cremation-stub photocopy. Board writes: do not clear the book for now. Call the number again and it is empty.

The extract can show he applied to transfer out, and asked that new claims stop. The extract cannot show which vessel is on the mountain, and it does not touch SouthHall. SouthHall's lessee is not him. He does not have to be here tonight.

Corner has a later half-line from Uncle Fang: empty number, do not dial again. Missing-receipt business goes to day shift. Night shift only copies this sentence into a recommendation reason. Do not clear the book for him.

Scratch paper folded four fingers wide. Ink in the nail bed.
