De branch register — Asheng line

Volume: PersimmonFlat stemTranscription after the 1998 revision

Asheng, third son of the De branch, born 1938. Lived at West River Town, PersimmonFlat 12. In 1996 answered the county CementPlant hire and moved into a GongrenRoad collective household. Died in the county seat, 2003. Married Qiu, given name Xiulan. Son Bonian, born 1968, now in the county seat.

This house's jinying entered Jinying Hall with the group in 2011. The register does not record hall numbers. Hall numbers are a lease matter. Whether Bonian later moved the jinying out is not continued in this revision. Readers go to the township board for the MoveOut filing.
