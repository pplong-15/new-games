Tongxi Lin register scan room — rules of reading

Room: west annex of the hallTranscription stops at 2019

This room only offers pages to read. Old damaged volumes and new electronic pages are stored apart. The De branch still takes PersimmonFlat as its seat. The East Lane branch split off in Guangxu of Qing. The household later landed in Chengguan. The register still keeps a side note, so people who visit relatives do not call two houses one household.

Same names are many. Asheng in the township is a milk name (ruming), not a generation rank. To find a person, read birth year and residence. Do not stare at the name alone. In 2019 two branch Ashengs were merged into one line. Errata is another page. Readers should read the split branches before the Errata.
