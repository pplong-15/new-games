2019 — bad merge and the split

Work pageMerged February. Split March.

The transcription desk searched the milk name Asheng, got two lines first, and merged them to save work. Residence wrote PersimmonFlat, then stuffed EastLane into parentheses. Issue put Bonian and Qiuhe in one cell. The print proof was sent back by the township board.

The split followed birth year: 1938 to the De branch, 1951 to East Lane. A LimeKiln 1944 line was scanned in at the time too. Registration was a neighbor county. It was pulled out that night. Not entered in the Tongxi volume.

The work page admits transcription laziness, not someone trying to take a niche. If a reader still hits an old electronic page, the two branch pages govern. Lease hall numbers on this page are blank. The register does not govern contracts.

When the township board returned the print proof it wrote one line: a milk name cannot be a household head. The scan room later changed the reading rule. Any Asheng must be recorded with a birth year. If tonight's duty machine still finds a ghost of the merge, treat it as waste paper.
