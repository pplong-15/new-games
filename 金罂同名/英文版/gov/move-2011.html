West River Town PersimmonFlat group GraveMove-in notice (extract)

2011-04. Earth-burial jinying in the PersimmonFlat group that were not kept on site were transferred, by household, into the county Jinying Hall. This office posts household-head name and birth year for families to check. Grave opening, bone inspection, washing, and like matters are to be hired by each household from a licensed service. This board does not list steps and does not introduce workmen.

PersimmonFlat 12. LinAsheng. Born 1938. One jinying. Transferred into Jinying Hall WestHall. Family present who signed was LinBonian. The old doorplate was taken down after GraveMove. New road numbers were not assigned inside the group.

Other households in the group: PersimmonFlat 8, Qiu Musheng, born 1942, transferred to East Suburb Cemetery, not into Jinying Hall. PersimmonFlat 15, Ma Guihua, born 1935, family chose deep burial on site, not into the hall.

The list only covers the one vessel taken that year: 1938, PersimmonFlat 12. Whether it is still under lease tonight, and the later same name on EastLane, are not to be written into this mimeograph.
