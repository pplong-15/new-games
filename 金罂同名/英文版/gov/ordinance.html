Tongxi County Jinying intake trial rules (extracts that touch night shift)

Old village custom called it jianjin, second-burial bone collection. After intake, the contract only writes placement. Jinying and later cremation ashes may share a book. They must be split by hall and niche. Niches may not be merged to save work.

Night-shift duties are written as three: open the book, match fields, issue a written recommendation. Moving cabinets, opening niches, and changing tags are forbidden. If grave opening and bone inspection still happen, a licensed service does them off hall grounds. This rule does not list methods and does not introduce workmen.

Two households with the same name must have separate niches. Clearing the book requires a complete receipt. Short papers: keep posting the old name, stop new claims. The duty badge lands on the recommendation form, not on the object.

This is township mimeograph, not a higher law you take to court. The night window does not take township originals and does not approve moving cabinets.

Binding stitch one needle off. Cover says trial.
