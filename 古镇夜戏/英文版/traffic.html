The coach leaves from Tongxian bus station. Last run 16:40. Miss it and you hire a van. Vans do not honor the package. They honor cash.

The town lodge is Huaixi Lodge. Half the corridor lights are out. Rooms whose plates cannot be read are not assigned to package guests. Your slip says east wing.

Flagstone Street is slick in rain. Tourism does not pay for shoes. After NightPlay lets out, someone may walk you back to the lodge. The walker is not the Stagehand. The Stagehand is on leave tonight.

There are eight parking spaces. Self-drivers leave cars on the empty lot at the town gate. The lot has no light at night. A Teahouse sign sits at the edge of the lot. That is where the forum took its name. It is not a place to drink tea.

To change lodging, go to the Huaixi Lodge desk, not WuChuang. The desk only takes ID cards, not ticket numbers. Ticket numbers are the troupe's matter.

Coach-dispatch insert. Tongyun handwriting. Thumbtack next to the timetable.

Last coach 16:40. Not the troupe's time. Driver Old Qian set it. His daughter is in year two at County No. 2 Middle School. Pickup at the school gate 16:50. Run another loop after that and you miss NightPlay letting out, so last coach is locked at forty. Miss it, do not call Tourism. Tourism does not run coaches. Vans take cash. They do not take the package. Forty into town in daylight. Sixty once it is dark. Bags extra, talk then. A Wuhan man haggled. Driver put out his cigarette before he left. Leaving he said that paper ticket, show it to a chicken, the chicken would not take it. Town-gate empty lot is marked eight spaces. Six actually park. The two on the north have been a walking tractor since June. Tires flat. Nobody claims it. Lot has no lights at night. People park under the Teahouse sign. That board is the forum name, not a place that serves tea. Do not ring the bell. The person who leads after NightPlay lets out is not the Stagehand. Stagehand is not here these two days. Tourism called Fu Sheng, who sweeps Flagstone Street, fifteen a trip, as far as the Huaixi Lodge door. He will not enter the alley. He says he gets yelled at if he goes in. Change lodging at the Huaixi Lodge desk, Old Han. He only takes ID cards. Hold a ticket number in front of him, he treats it as not seen. Station toilet needs a ticket to enter. Fifty fen. Fifty-fen tickets at the left of the ticket window. Rain, station to town costs another ten minutes. Ten minutes and shoes wet. Wet, Tourism does not pay. Two van drivers usually wait at the station. One named Cai, one named Lu. Lu does not take out-of-town accents haggling. Haggle and he opens the door and tells you to get down. Get down and wait for the next one. Next one is not necessarily still sixty. Sixty is the after-dark price. Changes, still cash only.
