noodles by the stage cost more and theyre not good. eat at Flagstone Street mouth. Cripple Liu's tofu pudding, pot comes out 5:30 in the morning, three yuan a bowl, soaks his own soybeans, iron pot, black ring on the rim, black wont wash off. his leg he busted fixing a house last year, not in a play, dont ask. after NightPlay lets out its only Li's noodle stall. stall sits south of the yard. pepper gets shook out of a can, he shakes too much dont blame him, six yuan, greens make it eight. dont eat the liangpi by the stage. that batch is held for the company. bowls get reused. reused till somebody recognized their own noon chip. dont talk opera with the cook. cook's sister-in-law is in the troupe, she does the porridge, also costume thread. whatever you say gets carried back. carried back isnt a good ending or a bad ending. just trouble. town has no late-night shop. you want soup, sometimes somebody sells lamb offal at the clinic door. sell or not depends if the butcher makes it back that day. he doesnt, you drink boiled water. lodge has boiled water. after 10 its lukewarm. tofu pudding sells out and they pack up. pack up early. photo people miss it a lot. you miss it dont blame Old Zhang for not writing the hour. he writes the hour and Cripple Liu still doesnt follow it. that pepper can at Li's used to be bean paste. washed. lid rim still red. red and people take it for chili oil. you take it that way dont blame Old Zhang. lamb offal stall isnt every day. butcher makes it back, then its there. he doesnt, clinic door is empty. empty, dont wait. you wait and the show already let out. let out its just noodles. noodles six yuan.
