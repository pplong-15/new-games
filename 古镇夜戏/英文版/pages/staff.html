ChengShi, troupe head, monthly pay, in charge of making the company full. Speaks short. County TV came for an interview. He answered two words: no talk. Not booked again. DiHou, Stagehand, paid by show, eighty a show. A Stagehand is not a doorman. A Stagehand sets the table, the lamp, and the soul-banner props correctly before entrance. Leave starts on the 18th of this month. The slip says returned home for a funeral. Not illness. No wording other than funeral leave. Do not change the list to sick leave. Change it and the medical-insurance end comes asking. Asked, the troupe cannot answer. Running Extra is not a fixed person. Whoever is written into the DutyBook counts. Out-of-town visitors have been written. Apprentices have been written. Apprentice Xiao He is not here this week. He reported to the county vocational school. Costume still hangs on east-chest second hook. The hook has a He mark he wrote in marker. Extra is forty a show. Forty cash. Collected after the show at the west-wing door. Collected, then a thumbprint. No clock-in. Tourism staff are not to be entered in cast and crew. If entered, it is still temporary. The word temporary can be seen on the book. To find DiHou, the notice board has leave. Leave is not expulsion. Expulsion is guild business. This troupe does not perform guild expulsions. DiHou's phone is off. Call it and his brother answers. His brother does not watch plays. He cannot say what a Stagehand does. The day Xiao He left he took a box of boot black. Accounting logged one line, three yuan. Not deducted. Second hook is empty. Do not hang someone else's on it. Hung, Xiao He will know it when he comes back. Coming back is a September matter. September is not this week. This week Extra is still not a fixed person. Whoever the book writes is who. This page is pay terms, not a bio. Do not book interviews.
