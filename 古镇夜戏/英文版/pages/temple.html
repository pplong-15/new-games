Laolang Temple Stele IncenseAccount

Copied by QiaoGan. The temple enshrines the Kaiyuan Emperor. The troupe calls him Laolang. Visitors call him a god. Wrong name, QiaoGan does not correct it. Correction does not stick.

The stele is on the west wall. The text talks lodging and corvee. It does not talk how to invite a troupe, and it does not talk how to send one off. People asking for a method are asked to leave.

IncenseAccount is logged by day. When NightPlay adds a stick, the account gains the last four digits of an out-of-town number. Last four digits are not a name. Names are at the troupe.

This temple does not sell tickets. Tickets are at Tourism. The temple is open until 17:00. At NightPlay CurtainUp the door is shut. Shut and people still come to match books. Matching books goes through Cultural Center transcripts.

The word Laolang will search to the stele page. Search exorcism and you get nothing. Getting nothing is on purpose.

Huaixi records

Copied by QiaoGan. Temple-affairs register note. Words from the back of a Cultural Center catalog card. A few extra lines came in when the page was entered. The lines were not deleted.

This temple enshrines the Kaiyuan Emperor. The troupe calls him Laolang. Visitors call him a god. Wrong name is not corrected. Correction does not stick. Open until 17:00. Clock-out at five. After five, IncenseAccount is not read on site. To see a transcript, book first. Booking goes through the Cultural Center switchboard. The extension is written on the office door. The door slip was changed once last year. Changed, still that extension. Incense comes in by the jin. Last year forty-two jin. This year through August, thirty-one used. The rest is locked in the west-room iron box. The iron-box key is not lent out. When NightPlay adds a stick, the account gains the last four digits of an out-of-town number. Last four digits are not a name. Names are at the troupe. This temple does not sell tickets. Tickets are at Tourism. At NightPlay CurtainUp the door is shut. Shut and people still come to match books. Matching books goes through Cultural Center transcripts. Transcripts are copied Mondays, and only if someone booked. No booking, that year's book is not opened. The stele is on the west wall. The text talks lodging and corvee. It does not talk how to invite a troupe, and it does not talk how to send one off. People asking for a method are asked to leave. The word Laolang will search to the stele page. Search exorcism and you get nothing. Getting nothing is on purpose. The plaque on the niche was left the third time the troupe borrowed it back. The borrow slip is at the Center, not at the temple door. Do not wait at the door for the plaque. The door is locked at five. After lock, do not beat the door. The boards are old. Beat them and the paint comes off. Stock slips are on the second layer of the west-room iron box, with the remaining incense. The incense is last year's lot. Forty-two jin in. Through August, less than eleven jin left. The short number is not written on this page. It is written on the stock slip. The stock slip is not lent out. Lent out, people treat it as a formula. There is no formula. What there is, is jin and sticks. Sticks are by day. Day runs to five. At five the key leaves the Center. After leaving, books are not matched.
