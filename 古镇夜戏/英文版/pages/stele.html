Stele-note appendix. QiaoGan copied from the rubbing-cabinet catalog. The cabinet is not online. What is online is this note. The note deletes sentences of the how-to kind. What remains after the cuts is below.

The stele is on the west wall. Flood in 1998. Water entered the courtyard. The stele was moved from its old place to this wall-foot. A corner broke in the move. The missing corner is not patched. Patched, it would not look like itself. Height about 1.4 meters. Width about six cun and a little. Header omitted. Gist: Pear Garden lodging. Kaiyuan Emperor enshrined. Corvee by troupe, not by guest. A guest who enters the lodging is treated as DutyBook. The phrase treated as DutyBook is explained by the Cultural Center as: if a guest is written into the troupe book, the guest is handled as troupe. The explanation is not a method. Rubbings are in a cabinet. Cabinet third drawer. Drawer face writes Laolang stele, one copy. One copy means one. There is no second sheet. Photographs are allowed. Flash is not. Flash on the stone makes the characters muddier. QiaoGan clocks out at 17:00. After 17:00 do not knock. You cannot read the stele through the door crack. Cannot read it, come another day. Another day, read the open-hours slip first. The slip sometimes changes to 16:30. Changed, it is written on the iron door. Chalk on the iron door. Rain washes it off. What concerns tonight is IncenseAccount, not the stele. The stele does not govern 2014-08-21. The last four for 2014-08-21 are on the account. The account needs an appointment. An appointment is not an on-the-spot open. People who open it on the spot are sent back. Sent back, there is no second sentence. The broken-corner face used to look west. After the move it faces the courtyard. More rain. The characters are shallower. The rubbing from that 1998 day is wrinkled. It also belongs in the third drawer. The catalog card in the drawer had copy run off. QiaoGan filled it in pencil. The fill is not the original card. The original card is underneath. Do not pull it. The stele matter stops at treated as DutyBook. How to do it is not on this page.
