Tongxian Experimental Troupe2014-08-21

Transcript

ChengShi note. Written on the back of a Playbill. Thursday leftovers. Gui Sheng gave two extras. Two used as note paper. Short. Short does not explain.

Stagehand missing. Travel list has an out-of-town number. Write Extra. Written counts as full. Full is CurtainUp. CurtainUp, no refund. Tourism likes to print tours. Tours do not admit people. Admit needs a name on the book. Name on the book is not a tour. It is DutyBook. People bring the WhitePlay rider. WhitePlay does not govern NightPlay. NightPlay does not govern WhitePlay. Do not mix. Strike is allowed. Three books here, I strike. Not here, I CurtainUp. CurtainUp is useful to me. Refunds are not. Ticket void is not a strike. Void is Tourism's button. Grey or not does not touch that row on the book. Row still there is still there. Do not show WuChuang this note. WuChuang only says curtain already up. Let her say it. Keys, second drawer. Wax, third drawer. Wax not needed tonight. People short, use the list. List synced over from Tourism. Synced, we take it. Taken, do not wait at the west-wing door for me to change my mouth. No change. Three books here, then come. Not here, do not knock. Door is shut. Shut is not empty. Shut is not opening. Back of this sheet still has half a Mu from Mulian that Gui Sheng missed in the print. Half does not matter. Does not matter, tear it for padding. Padded, these lines stay. Stay, work from these lines. Note is not bound. Somebody throws it as scrap. Thrown, tonight treat as no note. No note I still CurtainUp. CurtainUp follows the book, not the note. Number on the book counts as full. Full, their button changes itself. Button is at Tourism. Tourism likes to treat void as strike. Treat it wrong. Slip voided, row still there. Row still there, do not stand at the west-wing door holding a slip. Three books here, then come. Here, press them on the table, I strike. Cannot bring them, work from what was written. Written is Extra. Extra is also DutyBook.
