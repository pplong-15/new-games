The lodge owner does not watch plays. He said NightPlay lets out late. Hot water until 22:00. After 22:00, heat it yourself.

Outside the east-wing window is an alley. The alley leads to the road by the upstage door. Guests are not allowed on that road. Walk it and you get shouted back. The person who shouted used to be DiHou. DiHou is not here these two days.

Breakfast is porridge only. An old Playbill used to be taped by the porridge vat. The characters had bled. That bled sheet is not tonight's.

A guest wrote a phone number into the complaint book. The complaint book goes to Tourism. Tourism forwards it to the troupe. This page is not responsible for explaining that chain.

Checkout is 12:00. Whether a NightPlay ticket refunds has nothing to do with the room charge. Room money can be refunded. The play ticket cannot. The window likes to say those two lines together.

Tongxian Huaixi Old Town Tourism

Lodge desk insert. Boss Old Han wrote it. Crooked hand. Back of the hot-water boiler manual.

Boiler bought 2003. Burns coal. Chimney shares a wall with the east wing. Past ten he banks the fire. Banked, the water goes lukewarm. Lukewarm, you fire the coal stove yourself. Stove is in the yard corner. Smoke backs into the east wing. Backed in, do not slam the desk. Slam it and there is still no second stove. Alley outside the east-wing window goes to the upstage door. Guests are not allowed that road. Walk it and you get called back. Used to be the Stagehand calling. Stagehand not here these two days. Caller is Fu Sheng now. Fu Sheng is louder than the Stagehand. Does not argue. Keys hang on a red cord. No number on the tag. Number is in the desk grid. You take the wrong one, he reads the cord, not the face. Breakfast is porridge only. Cook's sister-in-law starts it at 5:30. Pickles extra, eight yuan. Eight she writes on the pot rim. Steam soaks it off. Soaked off, she says it out loud. Complaint book is under the glass on the counter. Guests have written phone numbers in it. Tourism collects the book every three days. Collected, it goes to the troupe. I asked Xiao Yan about that chain. Xiao Yan said do not ask. Checkout noon. Room money can refund. Play tickets cannot. The window likes to say those two lines as one. Saying them as one is their convenience. Not the same matter. Mice are in the rooms whose plates you cannot read. Not a haunting. Mice. Do not write it into the complaint book. Well water is for washing the face. Not for drinking. Drinking water is barreled, hauled from town. Late delivery, desk boils tap to make up. Pipes old. Rust taste. East-wing hall light wrapped in tape. Wrapped and still flickers. Flickers, do not report. Report and they say out loud they know. Light still flickers.
