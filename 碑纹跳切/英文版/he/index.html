After I stopped posting

Winter 2009 · note

Hands do not rub anymore. Paper costs. The back will not. Someone wrote to ask about Wenshan. I answer slow. What I remember is Dusheng. A smear beside the name. I took it. Chi-head left corner was already missing.

If the office still keeps the Bingyin pack, my ink is on the margin. Ink is not a stamp. A stamp you pay in. Ink is so I will later know which sheet I took.

That Grain Rain day>>

Letters went to the old address. Two came back. He opened the returned envelopes and taped them shut again, like he still wanted to send. The person asking about Wenshan left a number. He did not call. He was afraid that once he opened his mouth he would say Dusheng was wrong. In his hands Dusheng was not wrong. It was how the wall looked then.

After he stopped posting the calendar still turns. At Grain Rain he stops one day. That day he writes nothing. He only takes the old rubbing copy out to sun. After sun, back in the drawer. Camphor in the drawer. Camphor covers the paper smell.
