Paper damp that Grain Rain

Added 2008 · out

Bingyin Grain Rain. Paper damp. I took the smear too. Someone had tried a knife. Shallow. Dusheng still there. I did not dare touch the graphs. Touching graphs is not my job. My job is moving what is on the wall onto paper, including the pale track other people do not want to see.

Chi-head left corner missing. The break old. I pressed ink on the margin so this sheet would not mix with another house later. Later someone did come with a same-shape chi-head, asking if it was one piece. I said heads can look alike. Grinds do not. Graphs even less.

Those two graphs, Wenshan — I did not take them that year. Not a miss. They were not on the wall then.

The year of this add he already did not rub. Writing, the hand still wanted the paper edge. What it found was a keyboard. Dust on the keys. Dust made him think of stone powder in the rubbing pack. Powder that year went under the nails. Would not wash. Washed off at New Year.
