Iron-gate note

The 2011 job

Two trade rates

Pay follows the trade. Red-tracing and new-cut settle on two rates.

The shop sits east of the county town. Iron gate. Wall once brushed “Qingming red-tracing, book ahead.” In 2011 they did one job for the Ji house. Trade written red-tracing. Attachment shot the stele face. Shi Man handled it. The household-head scrap is on the back of the receipt. A staple punched a hole through the graphs.

This shop page has not been updated in years. Prices are old. Does not take grinding. The grinding line is struck on the list. The strike is shallower than the one on the stele.

To see that receipt, go left. To see how trades split, go to the red-tracing rate.
Iron-gate shop. Footer still has the old area code.

The couplet on the iron gate is from the year before last. The lower line is missing two graphs, like rain tore them. The booking phone still answers. Shi Man’s wife picks up. She asks red-tracing or something else. Something else, she says we don’t take it. Red-tracing, she asks Qingming or a plain day. Plain day costs more.
