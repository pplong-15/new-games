2011 copy

No grinding

The photo put the smear and the red in. Shi Man was afraid the household head would say it was not traced.

Date 2011-04. Trade: red-tracing. Pigment: poster red. Per household-head scrap, slot Wenshan. Pay on the red-tracing rate, not the new-cut rate. Wheel-mark noted “old, this job does not repair.” Shi Man signed. Hand ugly.

The receipt can prove that year they took red-tracing pay, and the red filled Wenshan. It cannot prove Wenshan is the name in the box. It cannot prove the smear was made that day. Shi Man wrote it himself: old, this job does not repair.
A staple punched the scrap on the back.

On the back the staple punched a dot off the Wen graph on the household-head scrap. Shi Man did not rewrite. He said it could still be read. The attachment is overexposed. The smear is still there. The red goes pale. When the office copied this sheet they pushed exposure one more stop. What Weng Tai sees is a copy of a copy.
