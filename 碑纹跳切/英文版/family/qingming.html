Red once is enough

Ji house oral2018-04-06Original

Those Qingming days are crowded. Bozhou called Chitu to trace. He said don’t make it complicated. Red looks filial. The day Shi Man came he brought a small tin. Traced, left. The wheel never came off the truck.

Someone asked why not take the old graphs off clean. Bozhou said taking them off looks like theft. Red-tracing looks like sweeping a grave. He thought that sentence closed it. Later the office wanted a projection. He found out what it closed was the people in the room, not the graphs in the box.

This piece does not talk about the cut. The cut is in the receipt photo. This piece only talks red: red is for people to see. After they see, it fades.

The wheel stayed on the truck because the truck still had another house’s job. Shi Man said the next one was tracing a threshold. Threshold red looks better. Bozhou still laughed then. After the laugh he gave a cigarette. The cigarette is not on the receipt. The receipt only knows trade and pigment.

Whether that threshold job was finished, Bozhou does not know. He only remembers the cigarette. The brand he cannot write. What he cannot write he does not count as evidence. Evidence he only counts the red on the wall.
