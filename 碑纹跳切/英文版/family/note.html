The stretch for the office

Ji Bozhou2026-08-19Original

Comrade Lu Zhi, I’ll say it straight. The ancestor’s name on the book was changed to Wenshan years ago. The stele face was changed too. Qingming traces Wenshan. Last year you projected Dusheng. My uncle would not speak in the room. He came back and cursed me. Said what is a household head even for.

I am not asking you to approve a rename. Approve, I don’t even know who to find. What I ask is do not put the old one up. Young people ask who Wenshan is, I can answer. They ask Dusheng, I have to explain the book all over again.

That Adu one, that was the house name. Whether it’s in the box I never opened. Opening was your reburial year. If you want to write Adu, write it on paper. On the wall I ask you, use Wenshan.

Please do not put Dusheng on the wall. Bozhou.

He wrote it, then changed one line: whether that stroke on the book counts as procedure, you decide. I am only the household head. I am afraid of Qingming.
