Night queue

The ones not filed tonight

The queue is an old table. Someone wrote PRIORITY in red, then taped it out. 117 sits on row three. Lu Zhi boxed it in highlighter.

Row one Shipu-Bei-084, west-village broken shaft, head missing, day shift said wait for a light match. Row two Shipu-Bei-102, clean cuts, no complaint. Row four Puyin-Bei-031, You household, chi-head same-shape. An old remark said do not merge it into 117.

Weng Tai dotted 031 with pencil. She is not doing the You stele tonight. She is afraid that when she hits a chi-head she will jump, then forget 117 is the box she has to file.

She did not look past row five. The tube makes the table look blue.

Someone drew a question mark on the 084 row. Ink bloomed it. The word clean on 102 looks pleased, like day shift wanted one easy file. Weng Tai traced the highlighter box once. Her hand came away yellow.
