Lu Zhi scraps

Three lines she dropped at afternoon

Lu Zhi does not like long ones. First scrap: 09:00 projection, 117, one line is enough. Second: I already returned the household head’s call. You do not pick up. Third: do not merge in that next-county chi-head. Last year someone stacked two faces on a slide. The office got shouted at.

Weng Tai finished the third scrap before she understood the pencil dot on 031 in the queue. She added one line on the back herself: the approve field is furniture. Tick it and the system bounces.

The drawer still has the in-store number for He Zhiting’s old rubbing. Ink on the margin. Lu Zhi did not write his name. She wrote “the Bingyin pack.” Weng Tai knows that pack. The kraft edge is already fuzzy.

Tea in the cup went cold. She did not refill. Night shift refill spills onto scans.
