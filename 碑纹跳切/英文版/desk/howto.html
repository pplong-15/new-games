Those patches on the picture

The old office machine still keeps an internal note from 2019. The writer already left. The sentences are plain: if an object on a scan also shows on another holdings sheet, click that patch and that sheet opens. Do not pull pages by personal name. The name field is for day-shift label print. Pull it and you get empty.

Patches that usually work: smear beside the name, red in a cut, broken chi-head, margin ink on rubbing paper, box-lip number. Decorative borders do nothing. A same-shape chi-head from the next county opens the Puyin pack. After it opens, come back. That pack does not merge into tonight’s slip.

Weng Tai’s first night she clicked borders for a long time. Later she only hit the pale track. The smear sits on three sheets. The run looks like one wheel that stopped.

The method page does not tick the remark for you. Tick wrong and the return kicks the conflicting fields back.
