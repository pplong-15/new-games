What the shift may do

Night shift opens scans already in holdings, clicks objects that repeat on the picture to match sheets, and writes a lookup remark. Night shift does not receive visitors. Night shift does not approve a change of graphs. Night shift does not merge two steles into one.

Last line is Lu Zhi’s add: 09:00 is projection, not overtime. File before you leave. A blank still counts as filed. The staff id stays.

After the heater goes off the tea room smells of plastic. The notice writes the smell as “do not bring scans near.” Someone read that as tea and moved the tea tin. The tin left. The smell stayed. Weng Tai only drinks cold at the desk.

The cold tea is leftover from day. It tastes like a thermos. She takes one sip and sets it down. Then she lids it tight. Tight is for bugs. In summer the office has small bugs. Bugs like the blank of a scan.
