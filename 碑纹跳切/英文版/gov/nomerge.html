Office Night Blank Same-shape Desk

Last year’s slide

Someone stacked the chi-heads of Shipu 117 and Puyin 031 on one slide, said the same cutter line, recommended a merge. One person from the Ji house and one from the You house came to the room. The office later wrote this: same-shape chi-head proves nearby cutter habit only. It does not prove two pieces are one stele. If the smears do not match: do not project together, do not box together, do not number together.

This page is for night shift, and for people who like to stack pictures. Jumping to Puyin from a chi-head is allowed. After the jump, come back. The 117 slip must not be written as You Shiquan.

That slide day, the You visitor brought a genealogy. No Ji in it. The Ji visitor said the chi-head is the old cutter line, and a line is not a house. No one poured the tea. The remark was written short after. Short so it could go on a wall.

The old remark was written after the shouting.
