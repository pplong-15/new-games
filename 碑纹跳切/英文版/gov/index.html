What the public may see

The station hangs notices and blanks only. Original rubbings are not behind this door. To see 117, use the night desk. To see the box, use the holdings list.

Complaints in recent years: projection put an old name on the wall, family said shame; the other way too, that the office took a recarved face as the base. The notice writes: the stele is the face. The epitaph is the base. If the face was recarved, flag it. The base does not follow.

The old same-shape chi-head remark hangs here too, so no one stacks the Puyin You stele and the Shipu Ji stele on one slide.

The station counter still sits at four digits. Four years, no move. The footer filing number is invented. No one chased a replacement. The guard sometimes points outsiders to this page: read the notice, then knock. At night the guard is off. The page stays open.

When the guard points at the page, he does not read it. He reads whether the visitor has a letter. Night shift has no letter. Night shift has a staff id and a nail.

The red bar does not hang originals.
