Red in the cut

Present-visibleStill there after Qingming

Red fills the new mouth only. Old leftover strokes sit gray underneath.

Cinnabar or poster red — holdings did not test composition. The red line follows the new vertical cut. It does not enter the deepest groove of the smear. The groove is empty, like a wheel passed and no one filled.

Red-tracing is painting graphs already there so people can see them. Recarving is grinding old graphs shallow and cutting new ones. Two jobs stacked on one face. This page only shoots the red. It does not teach a knife.

HOLDINGS

The red page does not list a price.

Back to desk

Under the lamp some red goes orange. Holdings did not test composition. The note says “do not treat as a cinnabar assay.” After Qingming the red fades. This shot is before fade. A after-fade compare was never accessioned. Lu Zhi said the budget was short.

A heap of red sits at the end of a cut, like someone pressed extra on the last stroke. The extra press might be Shi Man. Might be a child who later swept the grave. The child’s hand is not on the receipt.
