The list is a table left from the typewriter years. Someone added notes between rows in ballpoint. Inside pack 117: present scan one, Bingyin rubbing one, smear close one, red-tracing crop one, chi-head one, margin ink one, reverse one, box lip one, epitaph scan one, catalog copy one.

Puyin pack 031 is on the next rack. The list in red: “chi-head same-shape, do not project with 117.” Weng Tai copied that onto the back of her scrap. Smaller than Lu Zhi’s hand.

Back to desk

Rows where the ribbon went pale were traced in ballpoint. The tracer wrote “do not project together,” then wrote a near-word, then changed it back to project. Weng Tai saw the change and smiled once. The tube made the smile look false. She shut her mouth herself.
