The blot joins the Grain Rain piece he wrote later. Click ink. Do not click a name field.

He Zhiting likes to press a blot on the edge, then pull one stroke. Not a collection stamp. Not a name search. The holdings list wrote his habit as “margin ink,” so no one takes it for a seal assay.

This page proves the Bingyin pack went through his hands. It does not prove he agreed to later recarving. He wrote it himself: he did not dare touch the cut.

HOLDINGS

Margin ink is not a house stamp.

Back to desk

A shallow fingerprint beside the blot. Sweat. Holdings does not assay whose sweat. He Zhiting’s blog said summer rubbing makes a hand slip. Slip, press a blot. The blot kept his hand-warmth. Later the warmth became a dry crust.

After the crust cracked the ink heart stayed black. A dull black. He Zhiting said that was fear of mix. He had seen mixed rubbings, two houses in one pack, later a lawsuit. The lawsuit did not take a blot as proof.
