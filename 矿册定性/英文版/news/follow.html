Reader visit: Ning Guangfu was at Maternity that day

Society desk, added 2012-03-20

GuiXiuying came to the newsroom with the child. She said from the afternoon of 2011-12-09 through the night they were at county Maternity waiting on a birth. Ning Guangfu had Leave to sit with her. He did not go down. The evening paper wrote her husband dead. The stele cut him too. She does not want Bereavement pay. She wants the name taken out of people’s mouths.

The reporter matched the union leave copy. The date lines up. This paper will not pull the eight-name piece. This follow-up only. Someone told her “the Quota is already whole, don’t make a scene.” She swore once in the reception room and left.

Ning Guangfu later added incense at the temple himself. That ledger line is easy to read as if he were already gone. Gui Xiuying said he was afraid the elders would blame him for not being there. Society put that sentence at the end. The front page does not change.
