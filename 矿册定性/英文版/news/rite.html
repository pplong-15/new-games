Xiwa still held the Yao-god rite on Lunar18

Culture column 2012-01-11

On the first Lunar18 after the accident — the 18th of the twelfth lunar month — incense at the Xiwa Yao-god temple was thicker than other years. Zhu Changhe said all eight names must have incense; one short and the elders would not have it. Someone from the mine office came with a gift and said the resume-work review had been moved; hold the rite first.

This column writes custom. It does not judge Ventilation. The safety letter that said “review shall not yield to a rite date” gets one line at the end: some offices have dates twisted. Readers who want the technical page should go to the old safety site, not look for a Fan in this column.

An elder also counted Ge Wancai’s vow-repay incense as “among those inside.” The reporter did not ask for a delivery slip.
