Xiwa fatality list (from the temple stele)

Society desk, compiled 2011-12-13

This paper copied eight names from the Yao-god stele face and, following folk talk, listed them as dead: Huo Chengshan, Weng Erhai, Shang Xiaoman, Ning Guangfu, GeWancai, Lan Shoutian, Yin Fulai, Qu Peiyi. Xing Guilin and Tan Qiusheng are not on the stone. This paper did not list them.

Copy wrote Qu Peiyi as “dispatch, died,” Ge Wancai as “contract hand went down with them.” Lan Shoutian and Yin Fulai got no relocation note. Ning Guangfu was written “name on the roll, man did not return to the village.”

The desk cut “safety has not confirmed” for space. If the gazetteer office cites this list, match it to the in-shaft record yourselves. Xing Guilin and Tan Qiusheng off the stele does not mean they were not on the mine. It only means Zhu Changhe did not count lamp room and pithead check as units he could offer. Society took the short road. On the stone: dead. Off the stone: as if they were not there. These pages cannot determine death counts from a rite list.
