Files Eight Rite

Xiwa YaoGod still wants one Quota

Reporter Mi Wanqing 2011-12-12 09:18

On the third day after the accident, elders in Xiwa village said: the Yao god wants people; seven is not enough; still short one quota. ZhuChanghe told the reporter at the temple gate that the stele must carry eight; one short and incense cannot be set on Lunar18.

Someone at the mine office, unwilling to be named, said safety would only recognize those who went down, but the village was already crying eight. The reporter asked who the eighth was. An elder pointed toward NingGuangfu’s house: he was not in the village that day, but his name was still on the mine roll.

This paper filed on oral talk. The technical appraisal was not out. Headcount followed folk rite names. Readers later said a living man had been written in. See follow-up. Mi Wanqing stayed in the village half an hour. She did not go to Maternity. She did not open the mine-office mailbox. She wrote “name still on the mine roll” and filed. The front page welded the roll to in-shaft. Later she wanted it split. The page had already gone.
