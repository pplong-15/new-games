## Positioning

Working title: **Mine Roster Fates** (矿册定性).

The edition must read as an English-language records review *inside* a Chinese county gazetteer desk. The player is temp hire **Han Zhou**. Tonight they open archived sites, mark one fate on each of ten roster rows, and send a review recommendation. They do not approve the original register, sign for families, or write oral legend into the gazetteer body as a conclusion.

Registers stay split:

- **Hire sheet / review desk** (`introduction.html`, `office/*`): cold HR paper, short SMS, table chrome. Mou Yanqiu does not explain. Dai Houshan’s slips fragment and only the fourth slip writes the ten rows dead.
- **Safety bureau old site** (`anjian/*`): dry excerpt voice. Qian Buqi separates in-shaft counts from oral rite names. The ventilation page does not argue with “divine penalty”; it only blocks that wording.
- **Evening paper** (`news/*`): restrained lede, dateline, place. Mi Wanqing welds the year-end roll to the shaft. The follow-up is late and does not pull the old eight-name piece.
- **Temple committee** (`temple/*`): half-formal oral. Zhu Changhe repeats the 18th of the twelfth lunar month. Ledger lines keep donate / mailed / vow-repay apart.
- **Union file room** (`union/*`): issued-payment voice. Fang Aju recognizes three households only.
- **Pithead forum** (`forum/*`): 2000s board English. Qu Peiyi swears, then takes a line back. Xing Guilin guards the lamp book.
- **Mine-history lemma** (`museum/*`): encyclopedia note plus yellow-bar: legend layer, not an accident finding.
- **Old mail mirror** (`mail/*`): short labor-office forward; brick-yard receipt stays cold.
- **Cross-check receipt**: machine voice. “N files,” “no hit,” “not open to the temp hire.”

The player solves on-shift location, whether a name still belongs on an accident register, and what kind of source is speaking — not a Chinese literacy test.
