Farm-truck driver Ge Wancai, 15:00–17:00 same day, unloading red Bricks in the east yard of the county market. Slip signed. Do not chase the Xiwa-site load again. He did not go toward the mine.

The Incense ledger writes “Ge Wancai vow-repay.” The vow is last year’s mouth. The bricks are that day’s person. The stele listed him because it borrowed a ledger name. Wrongly entered. Plate number and yard code on the slip are not copied here again, so they do not glue to the ledger page. He later came to the mine office and asked: I repaid a vow, how did that become me inside. Labor did not reply. This letter stayed in the dead partner box.
