1 Rite day

Mining districts offering to a kiln tutelary, asking even wind and a quenched fire, appear in older gazetteers. The Xiwa temple is small. Incense ledger on another page. The founding note only tells the founding. It does not tell the Fan.

2 Quota talk

After the accident a saying circulated: “still short one Quota.” Staff note: “collecting a sacrifice” is a lore type. It cannot explain a Ventilation stop. It cannot write Leave, relocation, or vow-repay as death. If the gazetteer body adopts this lemma, it must drop to an appendix. These sources do not establish a supernatural cause.

An Incense ledger can show someone donated, mailed, or repaid a vow. It cannot show death. Staff also left a private note: using lore to press a family off checking Leave and moves will bring labor inspection. This lemma does not give a ShaftChant and does not explain how to “sacrifice again.” Readers who only see the word Quota should go back to the Xiwa main lemma for the idle year, then to safety for the three in-shaft men.
