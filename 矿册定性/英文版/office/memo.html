Drawer — four scrap papers
Go mark

1 Check on-shift location first. The temple counts units it can offer. Safety counts men who went down. Do not weld the two counts. Welded once. The draft is what that weld made.

2 Bereavement and the in-shaft list are one stack. Stele, evening paper, year-end Roster are another. Whichever stack sounds first, that stack lies first. Front page sounded earliest. I ate that.

3 NingGuangfu’s Leave from that day is still here. Union has a copy. Mailbox has the original. Back page of the Incense ledger still has the money he added later. A living man can donate incense. I wrote this twice. Second time I struck “deceased.”

4 Huo Chengshan Weng Erhai Shang Xiaoman — in-shaft. Qu Peiyi Xing Guilin Tan Qiusheng — on surface. Lan Shoutian Yin Fulai — already relocated. Ning Guangfu Ge Wancai — wrongly entered. Hand cramps here. First three slips only point. This one should not have been filled. Fill it and the next hire copies. You already turned to slip four. Do not say I made you copy.

Slips have no date. Ballpoint. 6/39
