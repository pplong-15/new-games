Ledger practice

Donate, mailed, vow-repay written apart

Deceased is another column. This extract has no deceased

12-20 NingGuangfu incense 20. Note: added after. The man came.

12-16 GeWancai vow-repay incense 50. Note: last year’s spoken vow, paid after the accident.

12-11 YinFulai mailed incense 10. Note: sent back from a worksite in another province. He did not come.

A ledger is not a name list. The living can donate. A contract hand can repay a vow. A relocated household can mail. If the evening paper reads “vow-repay” as “among those inside,” two characters were skipped. Zhu Changhe books money. He cuts the stele from talk. Two books that fail to match is ordinary. Huo Chengshan’s family never came to donate. The Weng household gave paper. The Shang house did not. This extract does not write fatality paper-money in the same class as vow-repay and mailed incense. Anyone who wants this ledger to pad an eighth fatality should finish the three notes: “the man came,” “vow-repay,” “mailed.” Then copy.
