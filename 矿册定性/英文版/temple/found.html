Founding note — half-formal
Temple gate|How the mine-history lemma notes it

Signed

ZhuChanghe, recorded

Rewritten from an older stone

Since Xiwa had kilns, workers have offered to the Yao god at the pithead. The year takes Lunar18 as the rite day, asking for even wind and a quenched fire. The temple is small. Incense does not stop. Village elders say: a kiln wants people, and it wants a rule. The rule sits with people. Not with wild talk that sacrificing one more man will let work resume.

This note only tells the founding. Accident headcount, Fan stop, Leave and relocation do not enter it. An office that takes this note as an investigation has misread the genre. Zhu Changhe does not read many characters. He had someone write this passage. On Lunar18 he still sets incense for eight names.

Belief stays with the temple. Case-closing stays with safety. The two layers stack on one day and easily shout. They cannot be welded into one sentence of divine penalty. Legend cannot close the case.
The note is for reading. It is not a headcount proof. 23/39
