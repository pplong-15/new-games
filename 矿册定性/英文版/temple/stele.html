Yao-god temple — stele face
Temple|Ledger|Evening paper that copied the stone

Stele sides

Back: donations

Face: rite names

No public rubbings

Recut in the twelfth lunar month of xinmao. Eight names listed from village talk, so incense can be set.

Stele face, right to left: Huo Chengshan, Weng Erhai, Shang Xiaoman, Ning Guangfu, Ge Wancai, LanShoutian, Yin Fulai, Qu Peiyi. Xing Guilin is not on the stone. Tan Qiusheng is not on the stone.

Zhu Changhe cut units he could offer. Not the cage. Lan Shoutian had already moved to Bailan. Yin Fulai was in another province. Ning Guangfu was in the county seat for a birth. Ge Wancai was unloading bricks. He has heard those lines. He still says one short and the incense will not set. Society at the evening paper made these eight a fatality list. This temple issued no correction. Back-face donations are another sheet. They do not list the dead. Anyone who takes the stele face as a safety list has come in the wrong door. Legend cannot close the case.
