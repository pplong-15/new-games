Memo

The evening paper wrote Qu Peiyi as a fatality. This table does not change wording. If a web page marks Xing Guilin in-shaft, it is only because he was on shift at the mine. One draft pass wrote Tan Qiusheng in-shaft; the check sheet put him back on surface.

Ge Wancai and Ning Guangfu are not on these posts, and not among the three in-shaft men. The surface-post table is a ruler for review: a person may be on Mine Office Road, in dispatch, or on the pithead slope; if they did not complete in-shaft formalities, the fate is not in-shaft. The evening paper welded Qu Peiyi into the dead. This table still leaves him in dispatch. Xing Guilin later swore at the web page on the pithead board.
