Notice deferring resume-work review at Xiwa Coal Mine

Shiling Safety Letter [2012] No. 2. Early January

Old site The stop-of-wind page

Windows out of place

By procedure, the Ventilation system is to be reviewed before work resumes. The mine office wrote that a temple rite fell in mid-January and asked the review moved, and asked that the investigation headcount “roughly match” the stele’s eight names, for local stability.

This bureau replied: the review date shall not yield to a rite date; headcount follows the in-shaft record and shall not pad a Quota for Lunar18. After the notice went out, the mine office and the temple still set incense for eight. The evening-paper culture column reported it. This station does not reprint that piece in full.

This page explains why the web draft likes to copy the stele. It does not explain how Huo Chengshan and the others died. Cause sits on the Ventilation page. The mine letter also attached “folk sources also record an eighth, useful for twelfth-month calm.” This bureau struck that sentence. It did not enter the notice body. If the gazetteer office treats Quota as a headcount in its advice, that is picking the struck sentence back up.

Letter copied to the gazetteer office. 14/39
