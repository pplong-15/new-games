Shiling County Gazetteer Office — industry-chapter hire sheet

Who sits

Staff id HanZhou. Mou Yanqiu pushed you onto the review desk this afternoon. Said Dai Houshan left the marks unfinished and then left. The Xiwa kiln chapter has to meet copy tomorrow morning. Before 24:00, all ten fate rows need a mark.

Xiwa Coal Mine has been idle a long time. The web roster is not the safety original. Someone later swept the evening paper, the temple stele, an old year-end Roster, and an Incense ledger onto one table. The draft almost all reads in-shaft. Outside they say the YaoGod still wants one Quota. One name short and the Lunar18 rite will not close.

What you may mark

Each row takes one mark only: in-shaft, on surface, already relocated, wrongly entered. You are matching where that person was on the accident day, whether they still belonged in Shiling, and whether the name should be on this accident register. You are not issuing Bereavement pay. You are not finishing a temple rite.

A temp hire only writes a review recommendation. The original register cannot be changed. Families cannot be signed for. Oral talk cannot go into the gazetteer body as a conclusion. Legend cannot close the case. Mou Yanqiu takes this recommendation. Not an approval stamp.

Where to start

The review desk puts the evening paper and the stele up front. You will probably believe the Quota story first. The old safety site, union Bereavement files, the mine-office mailbox, the pithead threads, and the mine-history lemmas are separate sites. The cross-check box takes one token already on an opened page. No spaces. Examples: Xiwa, YaoGod, Quota, HuoChengshan, NingGuangfu. Digits alone do not go through.

The roster panel can be marked any time, sent any time. Empty rows are refused. Send early and it follows what you marked. Do not blame the machine later.

Enter the review desk. Open the roster.

The paper says this itself

Shiling County, the Xiwa kiln, and the names above are desk names. Ask on the street and no one will know them. If a row sticks, open Dai Houshan’s drawer slips. The slips point. They do not mark for you. Do not carry this sheet onto the desk.

Hire sheet stays in the locker room. Do not reread it after you sit.
