A threshold splits inside from outside. The foot is to step over it. Sit, stand on it, nail, park a cabinet — alley mouths will shout no manners. Shouting is talk. Night duty does not close a case on it. 38/44

Net-user transcript · do not look up a real gazetteer · from 38

The entries were glued by a net user from a few folk extracts with the county name changed. The person who glued them is not a lisheng. After gluing, hung here for the Repair Command night duty to consult. Consult is not file. Filing is at the side door. The side door does not walk tonight.
