Do not step or sit

The old copy writes: guests do not step, do not sit. Sedan or coffin, raise the pole. Scrape the threshold and that pass is dirty. Dirty, you stop, you pick another time, or at least you do not let them into the hall as clean wedding guests.

"Stepping on the threshold invites ghosts" is swearing at someone. This copy does not write it as true. The night-duty board's do-not-step, do-not-sit comes from cells, not from ghosts.
