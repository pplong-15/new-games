He wrote two lines and rode off. Alley lights were not all on yet. First line sounded like he thought my hands were cheap. Second line sounded like he left a live path. The live path is the trial entry. The trial entry is not so I rewrite the rule. It is so I hit it once myself. After the hit I am willing to believe the board is real.
