What the last night duty tore and left

The hand is not Que He's. Smaller scraps. It says: the window liking red is not the fear. The fear is you following red. A trial entry is not a joke. What bounces is yours. The record page has one cell with an empty signature. Empty is not clean.

Another line inked out. Under it, something like "Qiusui." Last year's matter. The mailbox has it. You can file without opening it.

Bottom line: Jiang Mie's cabinet is the second door. Do not write it into the front door. Write it in and works will come shout. After the shout you still have not seen the crew at the door.

The memo can remind you not to merge cases. It cannot determine the rite. 11/44

The two inked-out characters I later saw in the yellow-page flower-car field. Qiusui. Last year's person. Tonight's crew borrowed her name. The borrow only shows an edge here. The body is in the posts, and in the key-box lining.
