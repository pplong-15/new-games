That front-door threshold

He-house front threshold is old wood. Preservation write-up: keep. Access ramp opens from the east side. Ramp is for coming and going, not for the rite. Someone calls the ramp offending a door god. That is mouth, not a Command finding.

Threshold is high. Lifting things, you lift high. Pole-skin scrapes easy. Who writes the scrape, writes it at night duty, not on this public page.

Second threshold is lower. Jiang Mie parked a parcel cabinet on it. Neighbors shouted no manners. The shouting is the second door. Whether the front door is dirty, look at the night-duty cells. 16/44
