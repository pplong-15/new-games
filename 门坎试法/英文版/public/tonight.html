How the window takes people

During repair the hall is not open for visits. Photographers, wedding studios, incense shops coming to register: this week the counter only opens the "wedding" cell. Funeral people came in the afternoon. They were sent to the side door to file. Side door is stacked with cement bags. Dust is heavy.

That counter sentence was overheard by family. Someone will think: hold a wedding name first, go in, then change to white. The counter does not check whether a name is true. The counter only looks at which cell you fill.

The night-duty board writes another set: tonight, record as funeral. The public page governs the daytime window. Two sets of words hang at once. Neither voids the other. You have to file. Which set you listen to, you match yourself.

This page cannot prove which cell He Lianzou filled. The register book is not on this skin. 15/44

Window note · do not match a real office

The counter auntie, talking to the funeral crew in the afternoon, spun a ballpoint. Side-door filing, three words, two spins. Then she took a wedding registration from a household that has nothing to do with the He family. An unrelated wedding cannot be Lianzou's shield.

That wedding registration is a Wu household, end of the alley. They do not share a threshold with the He house. The threshold shared tonight is only this crew. The crew name is borrowed.
