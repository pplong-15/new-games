Hall key is not on the public page

Visit-booking, you click, it will not open. Gray. To actually enter the hall, the key is in Gu Jiong's box. What the purpose slip writes, that is what is issued. What night duty recommends, day shift looks at in the morning.

This week's public line: wedding registrations first. The line is not the rule. The rule is on the night-duty board. Take the line as tonight's rite determination and you will send a funeral crew in.

He-house hall is still being painted. Setup people need to go in. They can. Under what name they go in, the counter cannot be bothered to ask. The consequence of not asking lands on night duty to write. 17/44

Key-room copies

Hall note · no keys issued here

Someone hears "can enter the hall" as "night duty ought to let them in." Can enter is key-room talk. Ought to let them in is recommendation-form talk. Between the two sentences sits the rule on the board. The rule is still there, so "ought" is not for the yellow page to use.

The gray booking button has been gray since last year. Gray does not mean the hall is locked dead. Locked or not is Gu Jiong's one key. That key tonight is in Lianzou's pocket.
