## Positioning

Working title: **Threshold Trial** (门坎试法).

The edition must read as an English-language night shift *inside* a Chinese alley repair command. The player is night-duty clerk **Chai Du**. Tonight, before 22:00, he must file an **admission recommendation** for one crossing: the crew at the He-house hall door. He is not a lisheng, not a key holder, and not the author of the duty board.

The player solves provenance / identity / chronology / rule-meaning: whether tonight’s party is a wedding team or a funeral crew under a borrowed name; whether the front threshold was already stepped on; how far a night-duty card may go. It is not a Chinese literacy test and not a tourist rite tour. The Forgotten-City hook stays: one world rule may be deliberately broken; the bounce leaves verified knowledge.

Registers stay split:

- **Doorframe / introduction**: Chai Du muttering; self-corrections; late-and-wrong fear. Staff id and the first targets leak out of complaint, not a manual.
- **Night desk / bounce / trial entry / returns**: cold, short, permission-bounded. This desk does not issue keys, does not rewrite the board, does not score the player.
- **Public repair site**: window-counter voice; dusty side door; ramp on the east; no tourism copy.
- **Key room copies**: only the slip, not the mouth. Tables and stamps. Gu Jiong will not reissue.
- **Duty phone SMS**: spare characters. Mu Sandao is afraid of being named. He Lianzou papers over. Gu Jiong is colder still.
- **Yellow-page ad**: red cloth, price off-book, dead buttons. A listing is not a bride.
- **Alley board (forum)**: messy 2000s floor English; heat; one truth is not finished by one mouth.
- **Public mailbox**: wedding-studio circular vs Que He’s letter to himself. Side line. Not required to file correctly.
- **Folk copies**: semi-formal oral rule (do not step or sit; wedding and funeral walk opposite). Then a hard limit page. Old copy cannot decide tonight.

## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

- A **wedding team** is not a **funeral crew**. Red cloth and a flower-car name do not complete a wedding. Hemp wrapping and coffin-bearing poles do not vanish because the public window prefers red.
- **Crossing the threshold** (a recorded pass at the menkan) is not **enter the hall** (admission). A key already issued is not “the hall work is finished.”
- **Stepping on the threshold**, **pole scraping**, and **stepping over cleanly** are three different actions. Trial-entry bounce for step or pole leaves the same verified mark (`verified.broke`); the bounce sentence differs. Clean is drill only. It is not a verified break and not a reason to enter.
- The 19:40 record cell already writes stepping. An empty recorder field is not a clean crossing. Empty means nobody would sign. Clean would have to say “stepping over cleanly.”
- **Recommend** is not **approve a key-open** and not **rewrite the board**. Authority is always recommendation. Tick approve or rewrite and the desk bounces or takes the wrong-road return.
- He Lianzou’s yellow page and the four-character SMS (“we are the wedding team”) are one mouth. They do not override Gu Jiong’s purpose cell: funeral-crew hall setup.
- Mu Sandao’s SMS proves someone used this duty phone to say the pole scraped and that a lift is still waiting for **He Shoushi**. It is not a signed witness statement and does not name whose pole.
- He Wanyang’s alley post (“hold a wedding-team name first; go in, then change to white”) is a leak that this is funeral, not wedding. It is not a key, and it is not a night-duty recommendation.
- Lian Qiusui is last year’s bride name. She is not in tonight’s crew. The flower car “Qiusui” and the overtraced pass slip prove a borrowed last-year paper. They do not prove tonight is still a wedding.
- The last-year rework mail (camera sat on the threshold; reel unusable; driver **Ruan Bo** only drove) explains why Que He wrote the board hard. It cannot determine whether a camera is still sitting tonight. Side line. Not required to file correctly.
- Jiang Mie’s cabinet on the **second** threshold (2026-08-19) is useful noise. **This specific connection to the front-threshold 19:40 step is ruled out.**
- Folk pages can state: do not step or sit; raise the pole; wedding walks in, funeral carries out; an uncleaned funeral crew should not be let in as wedding guests. They cannot determine who is in the hall, whose foot was on the wood at 19:40, or turn a recommendation into a key. “Stepping invites ghosts” is alley-mouth swearing, **not established by these sources** as a ruling.
- The public window “this week, wedding registrations only” is daytime counter talk. It is not the duty-board rule. The two texts hang at once. Neither voids the other.
- Missing a source is not innocence. Use: `not established by these sources` / `the public pages cannot determine` / `this specific connection is ruled out`.
