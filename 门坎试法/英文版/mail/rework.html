From: a wedding studio · To: Tongshi Alley Command · last year

Camera sat on the He-house front threshold. Follow-cam positions voided. Flower car Qiusui. Driver Ruan Bo only drove. He did not sit on the threshold. Command duty that night was Que He.

They asked for a reshoot or half the fee back. How it was paid later is not in this letter. This letter only writes "sat on the threshold" into the box.

Tonight someone is taking the Qiusui name into the same alley. This letter cannot determine whether a camera is still sitting tonight. It can only prove this threshold was held responsible last year because someone sat on it. 36/44

Old-file body · payment method is not on the page

Ruan Bo later called Command once, said he only drove, do not put sitting on the threshold on his head. The call was not recorded. This mail also does not mention what he argued. Many people argued. The one who sat down was the camera. Whether the camera is here tonight, this letter cannot answer.

Under "whole roll unusable" there is one more small line, pale from photocopying: positions include threshold close-ups. The close-up made the sitting posture clear. Clear, so nobody could deny it.
