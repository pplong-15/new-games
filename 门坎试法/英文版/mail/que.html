Written to myself. Do not send out.

From: Que He · To: Que He · last year, night

Next time someone uses a wedding name to come into this alley, record the crossing as funeral first. Look at the record first, then listen to mouths. The window likes red. Do not follow red.

Trial-entry mouth I leave to night duty. I will not be night duty's pen. The board has to be written dead. Written dead, someone will hate it. Hate is better than paying for another reel.

This letter explains why he writes hard. You can file correctly without it. Read it, and do not write his fear as iron proof of tonight. 37/44

To himself, not sent out · fear is not iron proof

When he wrote "hate is better than paying for another reel," he had probably already decided to write the rule dead. After it is dead, if night duty comes to rewrite the board, he has a reason to push the responsibility back. Pushing it back is not to harm you. He does not want to be the day shift who gets held responsible again.
