Inside the box lid

Gu JiongNot signed for the public

What the slip writes, that is what the key is. Mouth says wedding, slip writes funeral, no reissue. Wedding needs a new slip. A new slip needs day shift. Night-duty recommendation comes, I look in the morning.

I do not judge whether red and white are right. I judge the slip. Lianzou did not argue when he took it in the afternoon. He argued at night. People who argue usually already know.

Last year's Qiusui key: purpose writes wedding-date follow-cam. Later a camera sat on the threshold. Reel voided. Old slip still in the lining. Not the same key as tonight. Do not mix them. 21/44

Private note

Words inside the lid are paint-marker, afraid of damp. He wrote "people who argue usually already know." Not circular talk. Sounds tired of it. The tired person is the one who wrote purpose clear. Clear, then there is something to stand against a mouth.

East wing is not locked at night. Unlocked is not the same as anyone can change the book. Book is nailed to the table. Pen is in the drawer. He took the drawer with him.
