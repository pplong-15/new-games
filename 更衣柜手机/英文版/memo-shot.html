Back from under the lightsIndex

Alley guy rushed me to pick a plate. Sitting down the lights were white enough to sting. Mouth still tasted the noon dried tofu. He said same-day pickup. I stood by the shutter a while. Remembered Ma said don't shoot a new face this month. Feet still didn't turn around.

Pickup, hands shook a little. Not scared of night things. Scared once the date is written you don't get it back. I told him don't run a second print yet. Money later. Old bag he said still in the drawer. Paper has my name. Left from the March certificate sitting.

New one I shoved in the gallery. Walked back past frozen. Wind hit. Then it felt like I'd brought home a sheet that wasn't supposed to go out the door.

By the shutter it smelled like fixer. Fang Jing inside counting cash. Little book slapped once. I didn't ask how much. Dried tofu was Aunt Rong at noon, left by the back-room microwave. Heated it, didn't finish. Stool under the lights is iron. Cold to sit on.
