That glare on the locker doorIndex

Inside the locker 3 sheet, a patch of glue still stuck. Photocopy came off, letters printed into the glue. March date you can still trace with a nail. Face already mush. I wiped the glare with a sleeve. Wiped it brighter. Brighter it looks like bangs just cut short. Doesn't match the layer under the glue.

What got printed is that layer under the glue. The one I shoved in the album later, the door has no print of it. Don't dare pick the glue clean. Days will say I wrecked a file. Oil not mopped yet. Black along the shoe. Writing this then went to crack the door for air.

Air comes in, the face in the glare looks even less like the badge. Don't mix this note with the side-by-side one. That one matches dates. This one only says which print the door still holds. Glue gets drier. Letters get mushier. While I can still trace, write it. Shelf shot is later, different photo, no inside of the door.

Rust by the keyhole. Not new today. Key ring still has the produce-case key. Days used it. I didn't change the lock. Who peeled the photocopy, this memo doesn't say. I only wrote the glue still traces March.
