# English Localization Contract — Locker Phone

Full spoilers. Gate L transcreation for `更衣柜手机`. Chinese runtime at `/Users/Zhuanz/Desktop/新游戏2/更衣柜手机/` remains the source of truth. This edition does not invent plot, prove a haunting, or “fix” the Chinese story.

## Positioning

Working title: **Locker Phone**.

The edition must read as an English-language night-shift investigation *inside* a Chinese county supermarket. Shen Suo solves provenance (which white-bg is the health-certificate sitting), identity (who the store will still treat as “here”), chronology (2023-03-14 vs 2026-08-12), and rule-meaning (store inspection vs family face-matching)—not a Chinese literacy test.

## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

- Employee number **3716** opens the handset. That does not prove Bu Tiansui is in the store on 2026-08-21.
- Two white-bg files exist. **2023-03-14** is the studio file used for the health certificate. **2026-08-12** is a later attachment. Pixel similarity does not prove a soul, a swap, or that the sitter is “not her.”
- Fang Jing keeps plates and dates. He does not certify which face should hang on the wall. Someone asked for the March bag and said “family”; he did not hand it over. Who that visitor was is **not established by these sources**.
- Mi Tang’s copper-mirror / full-month / white-cloth rule is family talk, not store policy, and **the public pages cannot determine** whether the taboo “works.”
- The 2026-08-20 01:22 Mi Tang call (11:08) shows she still spoke that night. After that the handset sits on locker 3. That **does not prove** she left the county.
- Aunt Rong saw a produce vest. One glance, no badge number. That sighting **cannot alone prove** Tiansui was on shift.
- Wei Du’s mine / ticket-on-the-22nd thread is a side exit. Failed train-timetable loads **do not prove** she boarded.
- Alarm 6:40 still on for 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-21 is a near-answer (“she still meant to come”). Left-on ≠ showed up.
- Locker-shelf photo (2026-08-20 02:47) nails the phone and badge cord, not a person leaving town.
- Yellow-slip photo (2026-08-10) proves the store asked for a digital white-bg. It does not prove she filed one, and it does not prove a disappearance.
- Ending choice is Shen Suo’s word to Kuang, not a change to the real certificate, not approved resignation, not proof the folk rule is true.
- Hedges when a claim is open: `not established by these sources` / `the public pages cannot determine` / `this specific connection is ruled out`. Missing proof is not innocence.

## Retained-Chinese allowlist

**Target: zero player-visible Han.** No `.zh-artifact` wrappers. No operable Chinese, no Han-only search gate, no PIN or filename that requires Chinese literacy.

Allowed only if a later pass finds *pixels* inside photographs (this build uses CSS color blocks; no Han in those blocks):

- ZH-BRAND: storefront or carrier glyphs in a photo.
- ZH-FRONT: characters printed on a slip or bag inside a photograph.

Player-facing carrier, pad, softkeys, icon glyphs, calendar headers, and titles must be English / Latin / digits. Chinese queries are not required. Hidden Chinese aliases are not used in this edition.
