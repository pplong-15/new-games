Extract

Only the East Zhao quarrel. Meat share and dues not posted.

At the winter-solstice meeting, Meng Qiuping sent word: if anything happens to Shouchun, write her as the East Zhao paired spouse. Several of the senior line turned on the spot, said a second wife still living, written up, looks like seizing completion. Pu Jingshan hit the table: a paired spouse can be noted living; the cell cannot be promised first. The minutes were not written as a resolution. Meeting broke.

Afterward he changed his line and told others a reserve was easier to fight for than a paired spouse. Those two words, reserve, are the due-for-enshrinement in the later fax. This extract can explain why he was in a hurry to hold a cell. It cannot make a winter table-slap the basis for tonight’s deceased field.

Wanxiang was in the room that year. She did not speak. She said later: Ma wants the seat, the secretary wants the seat, younger brother wants to wait for a wooden tablet. She can perform dianzhu for none of the three. A person who cannot, later only stuffs a drawer and posts a thread.

Original minutes paper is in the association cabinet. Online, only these three hundred-odd words. Meat-share list is another page, not about tablets, not hung.
