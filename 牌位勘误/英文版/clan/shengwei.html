How the house side door takes them

A longevity seat is for the living. The wording does not say late father. Ink is often vermilion. It is not called a completed wooden tablet. The dianzhu set of rites does not apply.

Some people treat a longevity seat and a funeral-row paper as “a tablet either way.” The association already paid for that: in 1999 a neighboring-county house sent a reserve into a funeral shed. The next day the person came to offer incense and saw his own tablet name. It went to the county. After that this short note added a line: a reserve does not enter a funeral shed, and it does not enter the 17:00 red-paper run.

Pu Jingshan’s 2026-08-20 list still titled due-for-enshrinement reserve. The title was right. The line was wrong. Wrong line, and the house template will print it as late father. Printed as late father is a window matter. It is not this longevity-seat rule walking itself into the hall.
