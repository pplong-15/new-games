One empty-niche shot. No words, no tablet.

East Zhao: father generation. West Mu: sons, nephews, or a collateral line. Outside surnames, another niche. If a second wife still lives, a paired spouse may be noted living. She may not be written as a double death to seize a cell.

East Zhao is one cell. If a deceased father is seated, a shengwei yields. If a shengwei occupies, the deceased father yields. Pu Jingshan’s private note leans toward a senior-line reserve: “Occupy East Zhao first, lest the second wife move it.” A private note is not household rite, and it is not house rule.

East Hall at the funeral home borrows this association’s wording to line seats. Borrowing only. Real anwei follows the house window. The photo on this page is an empty niche, to remind that the cell is that small. It is not a scan of any one tablet.
