Hire your own lisheng

Some families treat the print window as a dianzhu window. They are not the same thing. Print is service issuing red paper from the intake field. dianzhu is a lisheng coming to the hall with cinnabar. This house staff list has no dianzhu officer. Cinnabar is not a storeroom purchase.

If a temp ticks “perform dianzhu” on the scheme card, the window bounces. That is not spite. After the bounce the original row still goes. To hire a lisheng, ask the day information desk for a copy of the roster. Three names rotate. The house takes no cut.

Undotted paper may stand in the hall. On a register, in a house niche, the old wording still does not count it as a completed wooden tablet. A records recommendation completes even less. Some people hear a recommendation as “the house already accepted it.” What was accepted is a cataloging line, not completion.

shengwei and longevity seats are not dotted either. The side door takes reserves and gives a receipt stamped “not a funeral-row paper.” If the 2026-08-20 clan fax went in the wrong door, that receipt will not appear. What appears is funeral-row red paper — the two slips do not look alike.
