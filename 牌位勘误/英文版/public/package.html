How a funeral-row paper is issued

The ordinary package includes one red-paper temporary seat. Issue follows the deceased field at intake, not the funeral host’s signature, and not a reserve list sent by an association. A host signature only shows who pays and who is the contact.

The dropdown has an item called due-for-enshrinement. That item is for a shrine reserve, a shengwei, or a later supplement. Tick it and the template still spits red paper — service is in a hurry, and often treats that item and a deceased issue as the same button. House wrote an internal slip in 2016: due-for-enshrinement does not default into tonight’s funeral row. The slip was written. The template was not changed.

Prices omitted. The paper itself is not an add-on. Hall, car, and makeup are. This page does not sell cinnabar. dianzhu is a separate lisheng. The house only prints.

If a name that rode in on a clan fax appears in the 2026-08-21 row, ask first whether due-for-enshrinement was issued as a deceased paper. Ask in records. Not on this site’s comment board.
