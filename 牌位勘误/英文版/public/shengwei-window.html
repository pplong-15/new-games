A longevity seat does not use the funeral door

A shengwei is a reserved seat for someone still living. Some households make one for living parents. Some make one for themselves. Color is often red ground, gold edge. The wording does not use late father or late mother. This house takes that kind at the side-door window, Tuesday and Friday afternoons.

A shengwei may occupy one cell in a house niche. It cannot be lined into the funeral-row papers going to East Hall tonight. Lined in, it looks like a deceased tablet. If an association sends “due-for-enshrinement reserve,” use the side door. Do not shove it into the 17:00 service print run.

The Zhai association faxed the house on 2026-08-20. Title carried reserve. If that name appears on the 2026-08-21 night funeral row, it went in the wrong door. That is not a finding that the shengwei itself is void. Whether a shengwei holds, this desk does not judge. It only splits windows.

The side door does not perform dianzhu. A living person’s longevity seat is not dotted either.
