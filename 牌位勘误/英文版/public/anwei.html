How the hall seats a tablet

East Zhao is for a deceased father generation. West Mu is for the next generation or a collateral line. Outside surnames do not enter the house niche. A paper tablet dropped in the wrong slot in transit gets a separate move-slot slip. Do not write it on the catalog card mixed with seating a living person.

Red paper still standing after the funeral window, not yet swapped for a wooden ancestral tablet, is called an overdue paper funeral tablet. The person may be deceased. What is wrong is still using paper. A wooden tablet wants dianzhu before it is a completed shenzhu. This house does not perform dianzhu. Undotted paper may stand in the hall one night. On a register it still does not count as completed.

One East Zhao cell cannot be given to a deceased father and a senior-line reserve at the same time. Who prints first, occupies first. If the print is wrong, records can only recommend a hold. The hall rack will not empty itself because of a recommendation.

Family who want to see their proposed niche: bring the commission number to the day window. The night bench is for temps. It does not call names to the public. The gate at night only lets staff and temps through. Family who come to shout: take a phone number. Day shift calls back.
