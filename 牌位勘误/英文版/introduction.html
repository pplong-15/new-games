The slip was stuck inside the locker

◯ Who called me in

I’m Wei Tang. Temp. The day clerk called in sick after lunch. Huo Mian rang, said be at the records room by 17:30. Key under the mat, he said. I felt for it. Cold. Dust.

He didn’t wait. I peeled the note off the locker door later: tonight a row of paper funeral tablets needs cataloging recommendations. File by 22:00. Staff id Wei-0821. Use that to get on the desk. Don’t ask for another password. Then a squeezed half-line, like he didn’t want it long — copying the print is fine. Don’t go looking for trouble.

I’m not a lisheng. dianzhu isn’t mine. anwei in the hall is tomorrow. What I can do is one card, six fields, recommendation. Whether it sticks is for day shift. After the window, the red paper still goes out.

◯ What to open first

Desk is on. Tablet row on the first layer. I glanced at Huo Mian’s handover and a ballpoint scrap in the drawer: someone wrote “my brother is still here.” I filed that as family noise. Noise doesn’t go in the deceased field.

Public house rules, evening-paper obituaries, the clan’s old site, those household-rite copies at County Library — bookmarks hang on the left. Public-mailbox password is on the same slip as the staff id, under the drawer. Burial bulletin has the commission summaries. I’m not here to solve a case. I match, then I write. If it doesn’t match I leave it blank. Blank beats writing a living man as late father.

◯ Overreach gets kicked

Card header is stamped: recommendation. Approve a pull, perform dianzhu, edit the printed paper — tick those and it bounces. I know. Hand still itches. Huo Mian’s “just copy it” sits wrong, but a temp’s name doesn’t print on the return slip.

Lijiang place names and people are house names. Don’t go looking them up outside. You’ll land in someone else’s county.
