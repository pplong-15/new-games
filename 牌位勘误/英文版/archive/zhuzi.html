shenzhu　tablet name and medium

Copied from the holding household rite　shenzhu chapterpunctuated by Cen Shulan

A shenzhu is a seat that can be moved, unlike a grave stele fixed in place. The face writes late father or late mother, writes the tablet name, writes the relation. Paper may be temporary. Wood is ordinary. The temporary is for the funeral window. The ordinary enters a house niche.

A tablet name should follow the deceased. A funeral host’s name should not be shifted into late father. Old hands sometimes found the payer written as the dead. A lisheng sent it back to be rewritten. An old Lijiang extract cursed that kind of “buy paper, write yourself.”

This copy is punctuation, not tonight’s operating guide. If records must match row 3, look at who the accepted deceased is, not who signed.

The empty wooden tablet has no words. Inscribed originals are not scanned onto the net, lest a holding be taken for some household’s true completed tablet.photocopy
