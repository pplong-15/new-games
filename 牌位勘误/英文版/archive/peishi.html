The surname on a two-person tablet

Old Lijiang lisheng slipcan be read against today’s hotline

Writing a deceased spouse as paired spouse: fitting. Writing a living second wife: must note “present” or “living.” Must not write both as dead and seize one cell as a double completed tablet. Writing someone already divorced and still living: the old extract calls it unfitting — the person is still at a shop, already dead on a tablet, the kind of fight that starts fastest.

If Jiang Ren is divorced and still living, writing her back as paired spouse is exactly what the old extract scolds. Meng Qiuping is living. If she is to be written, note living. If tonight’s sheet is funeral-row paper, the field may also stay blank, and wait for the wooden tablet to argue it again.

This article is a holding punctuation. It cannot prove who divorced, and it cannot replace the evening-paper hotline. That hotline piece can only show that someone came to declare. Read together, it is still a recommendation, not a judgment.old extract
