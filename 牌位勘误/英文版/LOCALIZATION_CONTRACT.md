# English Localization Contract — Tablet Catalog Slip

Full spoilers. Gate L transcreation for 牌位勘误. The Chinese runtime remains the source of truth. This edition does not invent plot, add supernatural proof, approve a move of tablets, or teach a dianzhu method.

## Positioning

Working title: **Tablet Catalog Slip** (牌位勘误).

The edition must read as an English-language night shift *inside* a Chinese county funeral-home records room. The player is temp clerk **Wei Tang**. Tonight, before 22:00, she must file a six-field **cataloging recommendation** for a row of **paper funeral tablets**. She is not a lisheng, not an approver, and not the owner of the red paper.

The player solves provenance / identity / chronology / rule-meaning: who the accepted deceased is, whether the printed face is a living man, which window the paper came through, and how far a temp’s card may go. It is not a Chinese literacy test and not a tourist rite tour.

Registers stay split:

- **Locker note / introduction**: Wei Tang muttering; self-corrections; late-and-wrong fear. Staff id and first target leak out of complaint, not a manual.
- **Records desk / returns / memo**: cold, short, permission-bounded. This desk does not move niches, does not perform dianzhu, does not edit printed red paper.
- **Funeral service public site**: package-window voice; split counters; Tuesday/Friday side door; no tourism copy.
- **Lijiang Evening News**: restrained lede; source, edition, “not on scene.”
- **Zhai Clan Association**: minutes / register / semi-formal notice. Not a real genealogy office.
- **County Library household-rite copies**: rule-stating; medium first; no method, no mantra.
- **Lijiang Local Board**: messy 2000s floor English; heat; one truth is not finished by one mouth.
- **Public mailbox**: Pu Jingshan semi-formal notice; Qi Man one-word “got it”; Huo Mian writes to himself.
- **Burial-service bulletin**: dry tables and an old circular. Not a courtroom.

## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

- A **paper funeral tablet** is not a **wooden ancestral tablet** (shenzhu). An undotted paper is still paper. Print color does not complete a tablet.
- **dianzhu** is named as a rite a lisheng performs with cinnabar. This edition **does not teach the tablet-dotting method**, stroke, character trick, mantra, or who qualifies. The funeral home prints only. It does not perform dianzhu. Shengwei / longevity seats are not dotted.
- **anwei** is seating a written tablet in a niche. East Hall anwei is tomorrow. Tonight’s card cannot empty a rack.
- **zhaomu**: East Zhao is for the father’s generation; West Mu is for the next generation or a collateral line. Outside surnames do not enter the house niche. East Zhao is one cell. A longevity seat and a deceased father cannot both keep it.
- The row-3 print (“late father Zhai Hesheng,” paired Jiang Ren, East Zhao) proves what the red paper says. It does **not** prove the man is dead.
- Commission summary 委翟〇八一四: accepted deceased **Zhai Shouchun**; funeral host **Zhai Hesheng**; paired-spouse field left blank by living second wife **Meng Qiuping**. That is intake, not a grave identification, and not proof of where Hesheng is tonight.
- 2026-08-15 obituary: deceased Shouchun, host Hesheng. The paper reprints a family draft. It is not an on-scene death report.
- 2026-08-18 grain-depot item: copy lists Zhai Hesheng as on-duty storeman that morning. It cannot determine whether he is still at the depot on 2026-08-21 night, and it cannot, by itself, prove the printed tablet names the same man — address and commission number must be compared.
- Clan register “living” means the association still has dues or a contact. It is not a hukou finding and not a grave finding.
- Pu Jingshan’s fax/mail (“due-for-enshrinement reserve,” occupy East Zhao first) can explain how a living name entered the print window. It is **not** a governing source for tonight’s deceased field. Due-for-enshrinement is a template dropdown, not a host’s handwriting.
- Jiang Ren’s hotline: someone declared she is living and divorced since 1998. That does not prove the funeral home already reprinted the paper.
- Tong Sanxi in the East Zhao side slot is a real mis-slot of another household. This specific connection to the Zhai card **is ruled out**.
- Tan Shuci is a deceased person on an overdue paper funeral tablet. Overdue paper is not “a living person seated.” That row is not tonight’s hook.
- Yao Bingchuan is the clean ruler: commission, obituary, and slip match. Do not merge his niche or spouse onto the Zhai card.
- 1999 circular + Huo Mian’s unsent draft can explain why he wrote “catalog from the print.” They are a side line. They are not required to fill the six fields, and they are not tonight’s governing source.
- Missing a field is not innocence. Use: `not established by these sources` / `the public pages cannot determine` / `this specific connection is ruled out`.
