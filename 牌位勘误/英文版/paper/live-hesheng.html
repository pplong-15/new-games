County grain depot cites flood-watch staff

Correspondent　depot office　2026-08-18　p.06

The county grain depot named staff for this flood-watch round. Storeman Zhai Hesheng stood three night watches in a row and collected a certificate in the depot meeting room on the morning of the 18th. Copy printed from the unit list as received. This paper was not on scene. The list title writes “storeman, on staff.”

The same page once planned a group photo. Night shift pulled the picture for “faces unclear” and left the text. The text can show that the 18th copy wrote this person as on staff. The public pages cannot determine whether he is still at the depot on the night of the 21st, and they cannot determine whether the same name on a funeral paper is the same man — address and commission number have to be compared separately.

Depot office phone is in the staff book, not public. Readers who want the words “on staff” corrected: go through the unit, not the death-notice column.
