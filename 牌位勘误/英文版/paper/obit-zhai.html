Death notice for the late Zhai Gong Shouchun

Source: family draft　2026-08-15　p.03

Zhai Shouchun, south of the Lijiang county seat, died of illness on 2026-08-14, aged 67. Home rites set for 2026-08-16. Then to Funeral East Hall. Funeral host Zhai Hesheng. Second wife Meng Qiuping. Daughter Wanxiang.

The draft end once carried “paired Jiang Ren.” Family rang on the morning of the 15th and struck it, saying they had divorced years earlier, do not print. The published piece follows this page. This paper did not see an original death certificate. Only the draft and the strike record.

Gifts, wreaths, and car times are not run in this column. Which day the hall does anwei: ask the house, not night duty at the paper.
