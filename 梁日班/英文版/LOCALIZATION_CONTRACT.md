# LOCALIZATION_CONTRACT　Huaixi Beam Desk

## 1 Positioning
English title: **Huaixi Beam Desk**. Chinese title 梁日班 stays on the Chinese edition only.
Setting: day clerk desk at a self-built house site in fictional Huaixi Town. The player hears one paper before sunset.
Register: V1 Yuan Jian stop-work (dry official). V2 Fang Shoucheng red paper / oral message (homeowner wants the auspicious hour). V3 Tian An safety slip (crew short sentences). V4 Lao Xun pencil (absent, broken handwriting). V5 Foreman Cheng / desk buttons (short, repeats “file before sunset”).
Promise: an English-language shift inside a Chinese worksite. The player solves which paper to hear, not a Chinese literacy test.

## 2 Evidence limits
- Yuan’s stop-work can show reporting status, not who stands outside at the auspicious hour, not whether the beam will crush anyone.
- Fang’s red paper can show he wants the beam raised at the auspicious hour and wants himself present; it cannot show permits are complete; the “send-the-beam / 寄梁” line is blank.
- Tian’s slip can show the crew wants outsiders off the yard; it cannot prove the homeowner is not the homeowner.
- The pencil cannot be today’s hearing.
- The oral message cannot prove the main slot holds two hearings.
- Permission is recommend only. Do not rewrite the contract. Do not sign for the homeowner.
- Huaixi, the papers, and the named people are fiction.
