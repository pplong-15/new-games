1987-07　one household

Wankou old gazetteer does not record the works name. The register does. Householder Rui Huaishan. Reason for leaving filled: work. Destination: Tongbu New Bridge Works, collective household. Category is neither adopted-out nor household-cancelled. Return-move column blank. No later note to this day.

1996, a works letter arrived, asking for a death close-out. Letter has no identifier of a body, no cremation number, no photograph. The clerk wrote one line in remarks: materials short, still treated as a move. Hence: this leaf can match the year and the reason of a leaving. It cannot stand as a grave deed.

If someone brings "adopted-out, 1962" to change this leaf, the register does not change. Adopted-out at that time would have gone through an adoption window, not a work window. Two sets of words that do not match are a later matter, not a matter of the window that year.
