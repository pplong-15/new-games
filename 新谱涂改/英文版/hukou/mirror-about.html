2011-08-19　after the move, no new pages chased

Can only match the old book

The old hukou site moved that year. This mirror no longer chases new pages. Paper still sits in a storeroom. On the glass, only some leaves of the move register remain. Whether a living person still breathes, the mirror cannot prove. The mirror can only match old-book fields: when they left, what reason was filled, whether the return-move column is blank.

After the stop, any return move, death, or split household will not write onto this glass. Someone matching the mirror against tonight's electronic book is matching an old reason, not who is alive this year. Taking the mirror as proof of a living person: the window that year already would not accept it. Still less now.

Collective-household rename tables, road renames: the links died early. What still opens: open-the-book, that 1987-07 leaf, the works letter, the collective scrap. No state emblem. Do not take this mirror to a real window for a photocopy. A photocopy would be expired paper anyway.
