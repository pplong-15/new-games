One letter from labor office

Signed: Tongbu New Bridge Works, labor office. Year 1996. Addressed to the old Wankou hukou window. Letter says: worker Rui Huaishan, entered the collective household in 1987, did not return to shift after 1993. The shift reported him missing once. Police receipt not attached. Now please close out as death, so the collective bunk can be cleared.

End of letter: no identifier signature, no cremation number, no photograph across the seam. One works chop. Chop text blurred. Seam blank. Clerk remarks still: materials short, still treated as a move. The mirror left that sentence on the register. It did not open a death page.

Hence this letter can show the works wanted a bunk cleared. It cannot show which ditch a grave is in. It cannot be used to tick struck-from-the-book. That tick needs identification of a body. This mirror does not have it. If someone on a genealogy takes this letter as iron proof of adopted-out: the letter does not even have those two words.
