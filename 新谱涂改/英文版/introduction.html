◯ Tonight

I am Rui Qiu. Head of the west branch, the one that paid. I did not want the chair. Winter Solstice meeting, they stared at me: money short, tomorrow they carry the book into the hall, west cell sits empty. I scraped it together. Hengpu Hall came to the house, measured the paper, drank two pots of tea. I still have the contract. Electronic page has to go up tonight.

Afternoon they dumped the page on the commission desk. I opened it — grandfather's line was black. Black like someone took a ruler to it. Small words beside it too. I cursed typesetting first. Then it didn't feel like a miss. A miss is a hole. A hole and a wipe are not the same thing.

I am not a genealogy clerk. Hall-upkeep, that other book in the hall — not mine to stamp. The desk gave me a mouth for a correction slip. Recommend only, they said. Whether it hangs is their business. Window shuts, it uploads. Uploads, it goes into the hall. If I pick a fight, west branch tomorrow: where's the money? where's the line?

So tonight is one job: old page, new page, the ones who moved, the old talk upstairs. Spread them. Then write. If they do not match, do not force a slip. Forced slips bounce.

◯ Doors I can still walk

Three doors still open. Inside the commission desk there is a comparison desk, two columns, you can spread. County library special collections is on overtime tonight — old book, scans only, paper does not leave. Wankou town board is still up. My father asked one thing on that floor. I bumped the thread later.

That broken hukou site, I'll go when I remember. Link is on the side of the desk, do not hunt it here. Text said the commission number is west-branch eight. My name is already on it. No password to guess. Contract and last year's bounce slip, I stuffed them in the desk side shelf. Not on this note. Stop flipping the same scrap.
