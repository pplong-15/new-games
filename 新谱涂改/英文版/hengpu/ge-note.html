Right hand of the post
Desk Old bounce

Three edges

Pencil. Torn. No signature. No date. Night shift does not explain who they were written for.

Top: both sides paid. Do the later one.

Middle: property bounces. Rite bounces. Do not grind the post.

Bottom: difference blank, I do not touch the row. Difference written, I only hang lineage.

One ink dot on the edge, like a thumb. Under the dot, half a Dong, torn off. No fourth scrap in the drawer. Contract extract, price list, receipt are not in this stack. Scraps sit on the right hand. Stuck, flip them.

In the crack, half a calendar: 23:00. After the hour, no date. Looks like a reminder to shut the window. Not written for the client.

Someone takes these lines as picking a side. The post does not. No branch name on the paper. Only the order of making a page, and the range of a bounce. Scraps do not explain an attachment year. They do not authenticate ink. Want the year, go to the receipt. Want the ink, go downstairs.
