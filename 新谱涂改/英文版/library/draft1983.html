Moved away, details unknown: the person drafting did not know where the man was. That is not already accepting adopted-out. Those two words are not on this leaf.

Appendix folder holds a borrow slip. 2019-03, the person who borrowed the 1998 continuation, West Branch Vol. II, for checking, signed Huaichuan. Return date written the 18th of that month. Slip has no "lost." No "damaged." The man finished looking, and only then came that supplement sent to the shop — that sentence is in the clerk's head. Not on the slip.

This draft can only show that earlier someone wrote him as gone, destination unclear. It cannot be used to change the book they carry into the hall tomorrow. Look, then close it.
