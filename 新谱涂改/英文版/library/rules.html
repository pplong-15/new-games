Wankou County Library Special Collections — Reading Rules

Holdings no.　Spec-RuiOn duty　You Shuang

Looking at a genealogy line, two sentences and that is all: paper does not leave the cabinet. Eyes on the words. Yuanpu is close. Special collections on overtime. Scans only. Original paper does not circulate. Photographs, register first. Anyone who walks out with a leaf is reported as stealing a book, not fined as an overdue loan.

House rules first, then the volume. Moved away: write one move, note the place. Adopted-out: write adopted-out to such-and-such a family, and an original paper or a hall-checked copy must sit beside it. Daughters, old books sometimes omit the name; a new revision must write them, other leaf, do not drag that into West Branch Vol. II.

A line painted in ink, this library does not count as a correction. A correction is a hung slip, a small note — not taking ink and wiping a person. People say "paint it clean." That is shop talk. Not library talk.

Anyone here tonight for the Rui continuation: read these rules through, then open the scan. The 1983 unissued stack sits in the appendix cabinet. Not required. Look if you look. It is only an earlier wording. It cannot outweigh a later published book.
