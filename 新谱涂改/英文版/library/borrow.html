Borrow slip — clipped in the folder

Staple holes　You ShuangEdge frayed

Main copy dated 1983-03. Borrower Huaichuan. Item: "Rui old book, west branch, one volume." Purpose column: checking. Return date the 21st of that month. No lost. No damaged. The 1998 book was not yet issued. He turned the old volume and the unissued draft in the cabinet.

Side note is later clerk pencil. 2019-03, the same signature borrowed the 1998 continuation, West Branch Vol. II, for checking. Returned on the 18th. Both times, that west household. After the second return, the shop received the east-port supplement scan — that sentence is in the clerk's head. The slip only writes returned. Not what came after.

A borrow slip can show who first spread the old line on a table. It cannot show whether a real paper existed in 1962. You Shuang does not explain these two copies. To see how the unissued draft wrote "moved away, details unknown," go to the appendix cabinet. To see whether the 1998 cell still has a name, open the scan.

A slip is not a notarized instrument. If the east branch turns "I borrowed it" into "the library accepted adopted-out," the library does not accept that. Borrowed only proves eyes were on the words.
