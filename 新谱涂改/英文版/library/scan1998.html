Huaishan's line has no adopted-out words, and no move word. Year the book was finished, the man was already not in town. The revisers still wrote him in the eldest cell. Old people said "the man leaves, the book stays." That is not law. That is what a branch head decided then.

A copy can prove this cell had a name in winter 1998. It cannot prove the man is breathing now. It cannot prove anyone wrote a paper in 1962. To match the electronic page, go back to the commission desk and spread it yourself.
