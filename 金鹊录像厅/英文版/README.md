## How to play

1. Open `introduction.html`, press Enter the hall.
2. Read the home notice, search **ExtraShow**.
3. One word at a time, from a page you already opened. Empty queries and two words with a space will not open a hidden page.
4. If you stall, use the closed-hall desk on Help (four hint ranks).
5. After Stub, OldHou, LuXiaotang, EmptySeat, and Tianmai are read, search **LastTicket** and choose.

Save key: `jinque-hall-v1-en` (this machine's localStorage). Independent from the Chinese build.
