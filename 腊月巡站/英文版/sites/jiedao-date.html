This jurisdiction’s Kitchen God send-off day — full text

2008-01-15　issued by West Gate duty room

County folk-affairs has checked: West Gate Kitchen God send-off is dusk on the 23rd of the 12th lunar month. Welcome-back is listed on New Year’s Eve night. Neighboring-province households often use the 24th. Shop pages on this street may not copy that. Culture hall has been asked to write the north–south difference into an encyclopedia entry for residents. That entry is not this street’s sales date for paper horses.

If a shop page changes the day on its own and residents complain to duty room, duty room forwards cafe assist for page-check. Page-check matches this text. It does not match what out-of-province relatives say. How a Jiangnan natal home keeps the day is a household matter. Written onto a sales page as West Gate’s selling day is treated as the wrong date.

Dusk on the 23rd follows that day’s county paper. Households that take a print down early are not stopped by the subdistrict. Shopfronts that write the 24th as West Gate practice are stopped. Shops such as Fushun that already have a spoken complaint: match the page first, then fill the return slip.

This text covers date only. Mourning-period red goods and incense-ash storage are in other notices. The firecracker ban is not among the three wordings.
