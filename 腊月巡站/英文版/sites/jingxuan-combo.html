Jingxuan　One-Bag Clean

Qi Xiaoman　how the package runs

One-Bag Clean is my name. Shrine ash, wall ash, window ash, one bundle. I bring the bag. Those iron bins at the stair mouth are the end. Household stands and watches. Separate bowl? I don’t. Extra step. Hourly rate doesn’t go up.

One room forty, two rooms fifty-five. Price didn’t change. Bring two pieces of sugar, I eat them. No extra. Deposit still ten. Pay on arrival. No Weixin. From the 12th lunar month, 22nd I slot empty holes. Which day you take the print down I don’t care.

Don’t take me for Jingua. That shop only wipes glass. Doesn’t touch a stove. I sweep. The ash-into-the-bag line is on the pin. Changing it is a pain. Pin paid through March. Leaving it up for now.
