Year-end sweeping and incense ash — full text

2008-01-17　issued by West Gate duty room

Year-end sweeping shall keep incense ash in a separate bowl, return it to the household or place it by older custom. Tipping it into a household trash bin is treated as unlawful dumping. Ad copy written as “One-Bag Clean” or “throw it with the dirt” : take the page down first, then see whether anyone has already gone to the door.

Wording that says separate bowl, return to the household, broom not into the shrine, is not processed under this item. The firecracker ban is separate. Do not mix it onto the return slip. If a pinned classified writes shrine ash as something that may be swept into a trash bag, match this notice. Do not match locksmith or moving noise ads.

Shops such as Chunni that write porcelain bowl returned: the directory is hung. They are not a complaint target. Shops such as Jingxuan that bundle ash with dirt: match the ad text. No need to visit the household first.

This text covers incense-ash wording only. Date and mourning-period red goods are in other notices. The culture-hall entry is common knowledge only. It does not replace this notice.
