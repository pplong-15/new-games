Shops retailing red goods in a mourning period — full text

2008-01-16　issued by West Gate duty room

A shop household still in a mourning period shall not retail Kitchen God prints, New Year couplets, window flowers, or other New Year red goods. If the white affair is not finished, red goods are an unlawful display for sale. Whether the seven-day mark is complete is read from resident report and the shopfront’s own words together. Duty room does not enter a mourning hall to count.

A shopfront saying “open as usual” does not offset this item. A red Buy button, red paper, and a price still up are treated as still for sale. Slow phones and people busy with a funeral only show the owner is busy. They do not show red goods have been taken down.

The first-seven count starts from the death date. The day seven is complete varies slightly by place. This street’s page-check takes still-hung red goods on the shopfront as the fact. It does not take a duty-room visit to a mourning hall as the fact. Shops such as Cuiji, if red cord on the counter is still tied, match this notice.

This text covers mourning-period red goods only. Kitchen God send-off date and incense-ash storage are in other notices. Sympathy for a bereaved house does not change the check.
