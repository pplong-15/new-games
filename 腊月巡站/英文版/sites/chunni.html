Slots from the 12th lunar month, 22nd. This county send-off is the 23rd. We enter the kitchen after the 23rd night.

Rules first. Incense ash goes in a porcelain bowl alone. Handed back to the household. Our brooms do not enter the shrine. Ash does not go in the trash bin. Some people want one bag and gone. That is not Chunni’s job.

Send-off day follows the county of household registration. Anjin West Gate follows the 23rd. A Jiangnan relative’s 24th we do not book by. If the household takes a print down early, we still wait until the 23rd night, when the Kitchen God is off post, before we touch the stove.

Slots run through New Year’s Eve morning. New Year’s Eve afternoon we do not enter. Leave the house the welcome-back. Prices are not written on a classified pin. Call. Subdistrict asked for New Year goods. Already answered: we do not stock malt-sugar for official offices. Directory hangs on the information station. That is a directory. Prices on another page

Footer stays. 2005 table. If it opens, it opens. Some people say eyesore. Eyesore still beats writing ash as trash.
