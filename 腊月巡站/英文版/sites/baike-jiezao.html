New Year’s Eve return, not a reason to take a page down

This county lists Kitchen God welcome-back on New Year’s Eve night. Between send-off and return the middle days are spoken of as an empty kitchen, and that is when year-end sweeping is talked about. Welcome-back itself is not a reason to take a shop page down. Duty room’s three wordings do not include “whose page omitted the welcome-back day.”

Some talk writes welcome-back as the day after Jiangnan nian-si. That is oral. This county does not set markets by it. An encyclopedia entry is not a stall. It cannot take goods down. To check a date, match information-station Ruling 1. To check an ad, match the shop page’s own text.

Intern note: this welcome-back entry was added in January, in case someone mixed New Year’s Eve with a site takedown. I refused to name any New Year goods shop.
