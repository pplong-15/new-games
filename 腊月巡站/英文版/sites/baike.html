Date split

Most northern places put Little New Year on the 23rd of the 12th lunar month. Old Jiangnan notes often use nian-si night. The county of household registration is the one whose posted day is followed. A natal-home province’s day is not a sales day. Anjin West Gate has already written dusk on the 23rd at the information station. This entry is common knowledge only. It does not change a shop’s price tag.

Talk of “officials 23, households 24, boat folk 25” stays oral. This county does not set markets by a boat-household day. An encyclopedia entry is not a stall. It cannot take goods down.

Sweeping and ash

Year-end sweeping is spoken of after the Kitchen God send-off. Old ash is not thrown with slops. Some places bury it at a root. Some keep it for the next year. Copy that writes “sweep into a trash bag” treats watch-ash as dirt. The culture hall does not fine. The fine is in that subdistrict notice. Sweeping entry　Welcome-back entry

Mourning period

A household still handling a white affair generally does not hang a new Kitchen God print, and does not paste red couplets at the door. A shopfront retailing red goods conflicts with this line. This entry does not name anyone’s second son. It does not name a shop. To see a shopfront, go to the shop page.

Intern note: someone asked me to name Fushun and Cuiji. I did not. An encyclopedia that writes a shop name becomes an ad.
