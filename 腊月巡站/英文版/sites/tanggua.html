Gan Laosan. South of West Gate crossing. Sugar pot on the inner sidewalk. I open on West Gate time. Before dusk on the 23rd the sugar is hot. South nian-si I don’t handle. Kitchen God prints, paper horses I don’t have. Don’t ask me which shop to close. Barley sugar 3.4. Regulars 2.9. Short weight, bring your own scale.

Someone from Lu Guifen’s office came for a crate. I said official offices don’t run a tab. Zhang Shusheng doesn’t wholesale New Year goods off me either. He doesn’t stock sugar for the subdistrict. I stock even less. This stall sells sugar. The day follows the county board. Anybody who writes me onto a takedown list looked at the wrong page.

Don’t put sugar on the optical drive. Fang Zhengping took two pieces this afternoon. Said night shift needs something in the stomach. Tab is on his wall. Not on a return slip. Phone 5503. When it connects, say the jin first.
