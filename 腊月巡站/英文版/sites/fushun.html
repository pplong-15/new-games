In from the West Gate market mouth. Red lanterns. I’m Ma. Xiulan. East Gate people keep asking how the north does the year. I still do it the way my mother taught. Saves a mess.

Malt-sugar by the jin. Paper horses by the set. Kitchen God prints I stock from that Jiangnan plant. Finer characters. Don’t lecture me on which day your county uses. My mother is in Jiangnan. Our house has always sent on the 24th night. This year’s bundle is packed for the 24th: malt-sugar, paper horses, Kitchen God print. Pickup on the 24th is fine. Early in the fridge is fine. Malt-sugar melts.

24th-night Kitchen God send-off bundle

Includes: two jin malt-sugar, one pair paper horses, one new Kitchen God print, a little barley sugar. Price　twenty-eight. I wrote it clear. Jiangnan old practice. Send on the 24th night. Some northern people take the print down on the 23rd. That’s their business. I stock for the 24th. Don’t come on the 23rd and rip my pack. Ripped, no return. List on another page

12th lunar month, 22nd — restock
Eleven paper-horse sets left. Someone said the subdistrict posted another day. I saw that paper. Not what my mother said. I still follow her. Married over twelve years. Even the stove facing I couldn’t change.
Pickup
Yell Ma Xiulan at the market mouth. Don’t come after 22:00. I have to phone my mother down south. Her memory’s bad. I have to say again: don’t forget the horses that 24th.

I’ll say it again

I’m not picking a fight with the street. I’m afraid you’ll buy early, take the print down on the 23rd, leave paper horses a day and they damp. My mother says: send early and nobody receives. You want early, go early. This shop’s 24th set is already up. Red pen on the tag. Don’t grab it as loose malt-sugar.

Phone 3981. If it doesn’t ring, bang the door. Old Jia next door selling firecrackers can hear.
