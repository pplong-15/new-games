Matter

The 12th lunar month is nearly over. Some West Gate shop pages still write New Year sales by out-of-town habit. Residents have named one shop by mouth. The rest were not named. Duty room is short-handed. Per the county’s spoken note on “cafes assisting patrol,” page-check is given to Star Glow night shift. Fang Zhengping has signed receipt. After You Shi sits down, this is the only job. Machine fights wait for day shift.

This job is only to take down shop pages that are still live and that match the wordings below. If a page does not match, leave it. Encyclopedia, forum, and the office’s own notices are not shops.

Wordings to match (already posted in duty room; these three only)

1. Dates must follow this county’s posted Kitchen God send-off day. Do not write the day common in neighboring-province households onto a sales page as this street’s day for selling paper horses.

2. If a shop household’s white affair is not finished, and the page is still retailing Kitchen God prints, New Year couplets, window flowers, or other New Year red goods, treat it as mourning-period display for sale.

3. Housekeeping or year-end-sweeping ads that write incense ash as something that may be swept into a trash bag and tipped into a compound bin are treated as bad copy. Pages that keep ash in a separate bowl are not this item.

First shop already named

Fushun New Year Goods. Run by Ma Xiulan, married over from East Gate. Residents say her page writes the Kitchen God send-off as her natal home’s day. Open this one first. Lu Guifen did not write the other two shop names. She said, “Match them against the notices on the subdistrict information station, then fill the return slip.” The slip is on the night machine desktop.

Tick the three shops correctly and duty room takes the slip; then You Shi may go off. Tick the wrong object and the slip comes back. Tick too few and no one is released.

Power on. Sit the night machine.

Attached: two lines in Lu Guifen’s hand

Fang Zhengping, do not let your night shift wave this slip at the old men playing chess. Look at pages, not people. Chunni I will speak to later. Do not get clever.

Ink still wet. This paper does not go on the street. Leave it in the Star Glow counter slot.
