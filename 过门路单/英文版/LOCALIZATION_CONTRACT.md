# LOCALIZATION_CONTRACT　Threshold Waybill

## 1 Positioning
English title: **Threshold Waybill**. Setting: night relief driver in fictional Shuanghekou, Jin Gui Yuan.
Register: V1 fleet print / estate SMS (forms). V2 Zhou Qiming joy slip (polite) / Zhou Qiping funeral slip (hard). V3 Lao Shen pencil / footer (broken hand). V4 Du Heng radio (short, repeats valid bill / keys). V5 cab buttons.
Promise: English in a Chinese night-cab. The player marks which papers fight, then presses by identity.

## 2 Evidence limits
- No paper is wholly true. Waybill identity decides which paper to hear.
- Joy and funeral share one hall threshold tonight. That is the contradiction, not a riddle about one man with two names.
- Pencil is a special case, not this week’s law.
- Footer order ≠ the whole truth.
- Permission is recommend. Do not stamp for either Zhou. Do not rewrite the SMS.

## 3 Allowlist
Stills with Chinese = ZH-FRONT + English caption the same night. No other UI Han.
