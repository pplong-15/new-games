Box: West-slope handoverCord replaced once

Wood-box corners worn white. The label only writes “Qingwa Nunnery old-slip copies, taken in after stop-incense.” The box holds this Qu copy, plus two others with no bearing on tonight’s ticket. Night window should not read them as one.

The knot is what Miao Shouxia retied last year. He was afraid day shift would stuff a stop-incense notice into the release field. The notice is on another page. Do not imagine a burning from the box label.

A box being here does not mean the slip is void. Void needs a release field or a burn register. This box has no burn register.

Inside the lid there is a cord groove, as if the rope sat a long time. A groove cannot stand as burned. Burned would have ash. Ash needs its own entry.
