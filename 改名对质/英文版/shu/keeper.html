Miao Shouxia scrap

Tacked inside the lidNo public lecture

Anyone who asks how to jump the wall: answer the cabinet does not teach. Anyone who asks whether they can marry: answer ask the window. Anyone who asks whether the slip is still here: answer not released.

The year the abbot took the reception board down, they wanted to keep the originals. The county only wanted copies. I register by the copy column, not by a mouth saying returned to lay life.

If Linpu writes asking about a same name, answer on another sheet. Do not lend this Qu-clan Hehe copy out. Lend it out and tonight will fail the number.

The scrap is a cut account-page edge. Edge paper is not a lodging slip. Someone photographing the edge as a release is taking a keeper’s tone as a column.

Vermilion is on the copy　Linpu’s letter is in the mailbox

He does not tell how to jump a wall because he truly will not teach steps, and because the cabinet will not let him. Not teaching is not the slip being gone.

Answer Linpu on another sheet. The other sheet is not in tonight’s box. Tonight’s box only has this Qu copy.
