The lines outside the glass

I logged what he said standing up. Not a signed record. When he repeated himself I did not tidy it. Confront uses the whole sentences below, not the attitude.

“Jump-the-wall was already done last year. Courtesy lodging was released long ago.” He said it and watched me, as if waiting for me to type issuable.

“The nunnery already stopped. How can the slip still count?” He welds stop-incense to release. The tighter the weld, the more it needs splitting.

“The genealogy followed. It is Qu Wanhe now too.” He slapped the household book on the glass. He never pulled out a genealogy book.

“The shift lead said names are aligned, so night window can issue the marriage papers.” Lan Huai on day shift only said night-check. She did not say night-issue of originals.

“The next county has a same-name receipt. Just use that.” He added this in the afternoon, like grabbing a straw.

“I already queued. You are still holding me.” This line has no column. Point it and he only pushes harder.
