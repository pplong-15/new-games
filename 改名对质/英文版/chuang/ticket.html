The sheet under the glass

Lan Huai wrote it in ballpoint. The stamped number-head has already faded. Number is 186. Matter column she only wrote four words’ worth: name-change receipt. Under that, a small line: also asks marriage papers, night-check, do not promise on the spot.

Caller field: Qu Wanhe. She looked at the household book this afternoon. New page is Wanhe. Old-page photocopy she stapled behind, written Qu Xiaohe. She did not ask for a genealogy. The lodging slip she left for night window to match against the cabinet.

Timestamp 16:40. He came again at 20:40. She told him to wait for the night lamp. Lower-right she drew a circle and wrote “three columns” inside. The circle has no gloss. The gloss is on the public-board notice.

This slip cannot stand as a release. It cannot stand as genealogy already changed. It only proves day shift pushed the person to your window.

What night window may write　The other sheet she pinned

Number 186 voided once in the day. The system gave the same number again. The number repeated. The person did not change. Do not take a repeated number as already checked.

The old-page photocopy is stapled crooked. Crooked still shows Qu Xiaohe clearly. Seeing the old name only proves he changed. It does not prove the genealogy changed.
