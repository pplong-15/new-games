What is in the bag

Only lines you pressed a button on will lie here. Take an empty bag to confront and every point gets pushed back, or you get told you have not taken it in.

    Cabinet, genealogy, notices, mailbox, old pages — each page has its own button. Do not invent a column on this screen.

    Some people like to bag the oral talk too. Oral talk does not enter the bag. The bag only takes lines written dead on paper, lines that can fight his mouth. Attitude, price, wedding nag — those will not fight a field.
