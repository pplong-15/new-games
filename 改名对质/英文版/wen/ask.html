Bunan misc-ask Four names Any paper

Nunnery stopped. Does that mean courtesy lodging is already returned?

Asker: Passing-through · views: some
The nunnery on the west slope of our county town stopped receiving visitors. The family lodged a name there years ago. Does that equal already jumped the wall? Need papers tomorrow. In a rush.
The picky answer
Stopped reception means the nunnery door closed. If the courtesy-lodging slip is still in a county cabinet writing not released, the window will not treat it as returned. Jump-the-wall needs a release. It is not about whether incense went out.
Answerer: someone who has seen the cabinet
My relative went for the license as soon as the nunnery stopped. Later the genealogy name failed. Bounced. Do not listen to a mouth.
Answerer: someone who got bounced

The asker is in a rush. Rushed people love to weld stop-incense into a return to lay life. Weld it, walk to the window, the window looks at the cabinet.

Misc-ask · not a cabinet column

“Passing-through” does not sound like a handle. It sounds like a real rush. A rushed ask cannot stand as a cabinet page.

The bounced person left no name. Better they didn’t. The window does not want a complete story. It wants whether the column matches.
