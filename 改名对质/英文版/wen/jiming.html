Can a courtesy-lodging name and a household-register name be treated as one?

Kid’s milk name lodged at a temple. Grew up, household book changed. Does the license still care about the temple copy.
The picky answer
Not the same one. Milk name, dharma name, household-register name, genealogy name can all be alive at once. Whichever copy is still hanging will stick a leg into the papers. The window wants a sync, not whichever sounds better.
Answerer: someone who has seen the cabinet

This page does not teach how to lodge a name. It does not teach how to change a household register. It only splits the four names.

Someone replied: use the one that sounds good. The good-sounding one is often the new household name. A new household name can walk through the police station. It cannot walk through a genealogy. It cannot walk through a lodging slip either.

“Stick a leg” is ugly. Ugly and accurate. Whichever copy is still hanging will trip someone on the papers.
