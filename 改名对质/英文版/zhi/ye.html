A lamp on is not a stamp present

Night window is open for checking copies, writing a recommendation, and blocking oral talk that obviously fails. It is not open for: moves, new certificates, proxy rites, proxy genealogy edits, or merging a next-county receipt into this household.

Lookup, paid genealogy edits, nunnery reception — day shift walks those doors. Tap those doors at night and you will see closed or forbidden.

Some people take the lamp as the whole section still being here. The lamp only means someone is on duty. The person on duty does not have the chief’s drawer key.
