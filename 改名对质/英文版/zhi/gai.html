How far a receipt goes

A written name-change receipt only proves police accepted and finished a change of the household-register use-name. It does not govern a courtesy-lodging slip. It does not govern a folk genealogy. It does not govern whether the license window will ask for more papers.

If tonight the receipt is written “may attach marriage papers,” that is night window making a names-aligned judgment for the license window. Night window does not have that pen.

A next-county receipt number cannot be filled onto this county’s sheet. Number series differ. Persons differ.

On day shift someone laminated a receipt photocopy and wore it to the genealogy office like an amulet. The genealogy office does not take laminate. Laminate only proves he went to the police station.

Boundary page · do not mix series

The series note is in small footer type: Lin-Hu-Gai is not Bu-Hu-Gai. Small type is easy to miss. What shows is the three syllables of a same name.

Someone did take a laminated receipt to the genealogy office. The office sent the laminate back. Return slip: do not take a police photocopy as a rename.
