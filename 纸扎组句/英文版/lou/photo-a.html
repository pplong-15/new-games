Tonight 20:51 | Category: stack shots

White-paper paper mansion. Red-gold showing from the seams. I do not know who wedged FestivalPaper into funeral use. I only know this is not the pile I am supposed to take tonight. When the shutter went, no smoke, no fire.

Jing Qiubai asked me later where the paper attendant was. I said I never lit. She does not trust photos. A photo can only prove red and white in this frame. It cannot stand as an already-burned return, and it cannot prove whose hand did the MixedIn.

LouShi signed: pile not taken, not lit. Mai's night helper wants to write: compare the invoice yourself.
