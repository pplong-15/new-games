Not tonight's return

Tea stained one corner of the sample. The account name can still be read. Mai Lao said copy the format. Do not move the words. Move the words and it counts as copying an old case. The old case has a number in the county extracts. Tonight has no number.

Last year's sample is clipped in the drawer. Mai Lao said watch it to learn the format. Do not copy the contents into tonight. Account name on the sample is another house west of the lane. Class is carts and horses. Status: not yet left the shop. No connection to Shao Ting or Shao Pu.

The format is: whose goods, what object, what was done, where it landed, how tonight stands, whether the person is still living. Six blanks. Authority only checks recommend. That sample was bounced once, because the night helper then wrote approve-burn in. Mai Lao marked the edge in red: write it again and pay is docked.

The old sample cannot prove whether tonight's furnace mouth opened, and it cannot prove whether festive and funeral mixed again. It only proves this desk wants a whole line, and does not take an approval slip.

Someone on the neighborhood board wrote last year's file as a mixed furnace. That was another night. Do not fill tonight's blanks with a sentence from an old thread.
