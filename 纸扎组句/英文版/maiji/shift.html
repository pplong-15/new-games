Last year's dock-pay notice is still taped on the counter glass. The name is painted out. The cause is not: writing approve-burn into a filing. Mai Lao uses that notice to scare the next hand. After the scare he still only gives the recommend column.

This desk takes recommends. The funeral host has the burn right. A night helper writes the incident as one line and clocks out at window-close. Mai Lao kept approve-right in his own hand. He is not here tonight. You cannot check it for him. Jing Qiubai can chase as hard as she likes. You can only put her chase into the bag. You cannot light for her.
