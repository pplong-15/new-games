The ink will black a stripe on a fingertip. Shao Pu did not come to the counter to sign. A-Dou collected it. The pickup field says shop clerk. It does not say funeral host. Who clipped this copy to the funeral order is no longer established by these sources.

Carbon oil is still on it. This sheet is not the funeral order. Account name, item name, summary: all another mouth. It can compare who paid and what name the money bought. It cannot prove the goods reached the shop door, and it cannot prove anyone in the yard opened a pack.
