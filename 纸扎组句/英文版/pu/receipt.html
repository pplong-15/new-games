The sheet Mai's cut for Puye

The clip has rusted. The receipt edge has a row of pin holes, as if it was stapled into another book. In the till drawer there is still a roll of unused party streamers. Shao Pu said do not open it yet. Wait till the lamps are hung, then decide whether to set them off.

Clipped in the till drawer. Account name Shao Pu. Summary FestivalPaper. Lines: LionHead one pair, storefront lamps four. Amount omitted. Issued by Mai's. Date is tonight's window.

A receipt can prove money and item name. It cannot prove the goods reached the glass case, and even less that they were stacked into a funeral pile. To compare the stack, look at the yard. To compare the person, look at the shop group.

The funeral-order sheet is not in this shop. Jing Qiubai does not come to Puye for a receipt. The two papers only meet at Mai's counter.

If someone copies this receipt's item name into a funeral-use column, Mai Lao will bounce it. If a night hand fills PaperAttendant under Shao Pu's name, that also will not compare.
