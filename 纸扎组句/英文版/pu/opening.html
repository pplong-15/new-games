A chosen day to open

The bottom edge of the notice is taped down. Dust in the tape. A lane kid drew a circle on the glass with a finger. The circle goes around the words no firecrackers. Shao Pu did not wipe it. He said wipe it and someone will ask why not.

Puye Stationery chooses this week to hang lamps. No firecrackers, so the funeral shed at the lane mouth will not hear. Owner ShaoPu wrote: elder brother's matter, keep away by the household rule. The storefront still has to be bright. Lamps and a LionHead already ordered from Mai's. Summary written as FestivalPaper, not funeral use.

Someone told him to change the date. He said rent already started. Change again and he pays half a month empty. The notice is inside the glass. Ink not dry. A finger smeared one corner of the characters.

This page is what the shop pasted itself. It can show he means to open. It cannot prove the goods already arrived, and it cannot prove the yard's red-gold pile is his. To compare goods, look at the receipt and the invoice.
