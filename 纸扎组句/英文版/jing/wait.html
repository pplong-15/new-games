The shed lamp is still on

Jing Qiubai Tonight 17:10Forward

I sat in the shed till dark. Straw mat damp. Knees hurt. People coming to offer incense turned PaperAttendant into a refrain. I answered till I was sick of myself. They asked if the paper attendant had arrived. I said Mai's said it was stacked. Lou Shi's photo still left a hole in me.

If we do not burn this piece at fifth-seven, the elders will say I made the name sit low. I am not trying to go against Pu. He keeps away, he keeps away. His shop, his lamps, do not enter my stack.

Someone told me: photo is blurry, maybe the paper attendant is underneath. Maybe. I still want Mai's to write one line. Missing or not. Burned or not. Writing AttendantBurned is fine too, if the furnace mouth admits it. If the mouth does not admit it, do not write it.

I am waiting for one line I can read to the household. Not an approval slip. I cannot issue an approval slip myself. A night helper even less.
