Ting is gone. Pu is still keeping away

Jing Qiubai Last night 22:05Forward

ShaoTing is the elder. When Ting was here he cursed Pu for not knowing how to live. Pu cursed back that Ting only held a face. After the cursing they were still brothers. ShaoPu is the younger. The household told the younger to keep away. Do not enter the shed. Do not open the wake. That is a rule. It is not writing him as a person who should be sent.

Pu wants to open a shop. I tried to stop him. He said rent already started. Mai's takes both ends. I know. I only want them not to send the shop's red-gold onto the funeral stack. If they send it, the photo becomes my handle for cursing.

Someone muttered in private that the younger should also have a paper figure. I will not allow it. A living person is not assigned funeral use. His name is on the stationery-shop glass. Not on my order.

This is how the household ranks it. It can show who keeps away and who is funeral host. It cannot determine whether the goods mixed. Mixed or not: look at the stack and the invoice.
