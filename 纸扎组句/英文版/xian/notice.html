A window is a date, not a permit

Burning This page Application Periodical

Window extract

Fifth-seven is one common window for burning paper figures. Days circled on a calendar are often miscopied as already completed. Miscopy that into a filing and the furnace mouth will not recognize the circle. It recognizes the table. A window means an application may be made on that day. It does not mean approval has been granted, and even less that a burn is already finished. Writing a window as already burned is treating a calendar as a return slip.

Shao Ting's fifth-seven is tomorrow. Tonight is the eve. Waiting-burn on the eve is not a required burn tonight. Opening-day pieces have no window they may enter. Opening-day pieces entering a funeral furnace are recorded under split-furnace.

This page only concerns date custom. It does not concern what color Mai's yard stacked. Color is to be seen on the stack.

Someone copied eve as the day itself. Off by one day. Off by one day, the furnace table and the funeral host's chase will bite each other. After they bite, the table still governs.
