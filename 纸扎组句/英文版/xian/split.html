Split-furnace notice extract

Winter 2019. A shop number placed opening-day lamps and funeral-use pieces in one furnace. The extract-desk night clerk wrote split in red on the header. The character is crooked. The crooked character was later printed into the copy. Nobody corrected it. The extract desk copied one line: festive crafts and funeral sets are split by time and by furnace. Burning festive goods first and funeral goods after is still a recorded breach. Same furnace is a misplaced lot.

What the neighborhood calls MixedFurnace is an old complaint label. This extract records only that the complaint was found established and the shop number was ordered to correct. It does not record paper figures speaking, and it does not record persons swapped in the underworld. Whether Mai's mixed again tonight is not established by these sources. What can be established is that the rule is still posted.

Lou Shi later formed the habit of photographing before taking a pile. That habit relates to this line. A photograph is still a frame. It is not a permit to light.
