Those spoken July taboos

Meiwan Night Read2023-08-09Notes

The street likes to say no weddings in July, no moving house. The registry office has no such rule. Whether a restaurant takes a table is the owner fearing talk, not the law.

People run to incense shops asking if they can book a banquet. An incense shop is not a banquet desk. Writing a spoken taboo as "marriage not allowed" is lifting chat into a stamp.

This is Night Read's own idle copy. There is no procedure in it for opening a ghost gate. There is also no house's ledger. Read it as chat. If you take it as true, ask the old people in your own house. Do not ask web-order night shift.

What Zhongyuan week can actually go on a notice is the stretches that ban burning and lamp release. The rest is mouth.

A reader wanted me to list "July can / cannot." I will not. List it and it gets copied into an incense-shop note and becomes a fake stamp. Wedding dates go to the registry office. Moving house goes to the contract you sign.
