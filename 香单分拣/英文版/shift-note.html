Back to queue

Shot on the afternoon of the 13th

Qu Nan's hand is ugly. Ancestral seat: one table. Solitary-soul: one table. Lamp offering is not niche entry. Street burn-bans: hold. Do not light a fire in the alley for anyone.

A fainter line under that: the Ji house was changed last year. Ledger in iron cabinet, second slot. He did not write who changed it.

A corner of last year's city-management fine is taped at the edge. Household name blacked out. Three words left: Nanmen Alley. He wants night shift to check the notice. Do not call and wake him.

Key in the drawer. Tea-scum bowl unwashed. On the way out he said the queue will grow by itself. Nobody has to watch the door.

Half a strip of tape along the bottom. Used to hold Huo Mai's name. Torn off. He does not like night shift treating a private log as shop rule. The fine slip is still the one he had someone trace.

The tea-scum bowl he said he would wash in the morning. Do not wash it. Washed means you touched his cup. The key opens the drawer and the cabinet. Not the shop shutter.

The marker is almost dry. He wrote the burn-ban once in red. The red is fainter. Black can still write the ancestral-seat line.
