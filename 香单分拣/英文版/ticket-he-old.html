Last year, June 28. Same He Peinian niche, late father He Lishan, table ancestral seat, death anniversary June 3. After morning shift printed he came in for incense. The signature is his.

Receipt remark: no name change, no merged niche, no request to write a side branch in. Money cleared. This copy only shows the He house walked this once last year. It does not establish He Lishan's life, and it cannot be fitted onto another house.

Qu Nan checked the stub corner. Mi Sui wrote "regular." If night shift holds this ticket she will probably ask in the morning whether your eyes blurred.

He Lishan's death anniversary has not moved in six years. A side branch once asked about merging niches. He Peinian said "no merge." That sentence is not on this copy. It is in Qu Nan's head. Night shift does not invent it.

Oil spot on the lower right. Mi Sui said incense oil, not ink. This sheet only proves the He house walked a familiar road once. Do not treat the oil as hidden writing.
