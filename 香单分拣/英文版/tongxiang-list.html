Ji Shoucheng2014Died away, DongguanNone

Each year around July 12 the association prepares unclaimed-dead clothing for him. No house branch has come forward to claim the rite. Someone out of province has written him as late father. The roster is not deleted, and he is not rewritten as having an heir.

This table cannot prove the details of a Dongguan death registration. It can only prove the association account once had this line. People who want to take him into an ancestral niche should ask their own house. Do not come here for a stamp.

After 2014, no house branch came forward to claim the rite. If the association misses a year, the clothing stops for that year. The roster remains. A stopped year is not a strike-off, and still less a rewrite into having an heir.
