Notice text Sites

Four questions residents keep asking

Q: Does an incense-shop agency order count as approval. A: No. An agency order is a sale of incense and candles. The stamp that approves a fire is not at the incense shop.

Q: The old one cannot walk. Can we make do at the alley mouth. A: No. The notice follows the stretch, not the legs.

Q: Does the designated site require the deceased's name. A: Staff only look for a water bucket. They do not register a genealogy.

Q: Is "no moving house in July" banned here too. A: That is spoken custom. This column does not govern moving house or wedding dates. It governs open flame.

How solitary-soul and ancestral seat are split is not something a Q&A page can explain. Ask your own house, or ask the agency back office for the ledger. Do not come to a city-management window asking for a "yes."

Weng Shiqiao does not take incense-shop night-shift calls. The notice is signed in his name. Staff at the site are street-office people. Writing an inquiry as "already reported and approved by city management" is false.

Bring water to the designated site. If an incense shop writes "alley mouth already arranged" on a receipt, one more citation will sit on the shop sign.
