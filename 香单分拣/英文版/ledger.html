This shop keeps a handwritten ledger for regulars. It is not a genealogy office. It is not a household register. The index only opens the houses tonight can use.

Tong house(none)—Lamps only

The ledger shows this shop once set a table by these characters. It does not prove a shrine hall recognized them, and night shift cannot change a line in it.

The Tong house has no ancestral-seat slip because they only came to renew lamps. Do not read a blank row as a place you may fill any name. The Ji-house row has a fine slip. Open it and you can see who "not entered in the niche" was written for.
