What the three fields do

Release = morning shift may print. Hold = do not print yet; Mi Sui calls back in the morning. Neither tablet slot moves.

Table assignment is for the people who set tables. Ancestral seat faces the ancestral niche. Solitary-soul table is opened separately. It does not enter that family's slot. Lamp offering hangs at the temple only. It is not written into a niche. No table = this shop will not proxy that item.

The box below is for morning shift. Dates, names, why you held it. Do not write moods. You will not be here tomorrow. Mi Sui answers from the words.

No banquets. Do not ask this shop whether July weddings are allowed. Hold that kind of inquiry. Do not write it as an approval.

Words in the note field go on the receipt. "Look again" counts as blank. Write "Kaiping does not agree" or "June 19 stays," and morning shift knows which phone to dial first.

Release is not approval of a rite. Hold is not a fine. Change table only changes the table card morning shift prints. Do not tape this page to the shop glass.

Printer ribbon is in the third cabinet slot. Morning shift changes it. Night shift does not open a test print. One voided test sheet and Qu Nan already said it once.
