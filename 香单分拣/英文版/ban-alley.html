Notice Q&A

Why the Nanmen Alley mouth is lined in red

The empty patch sits against two window sills. Paper ash goes in the windows. The staff point was moved to the old grain depot because there is a tap there, not because of feng shui.

The map does not write house numbers. If a resident matches the wrong door, this column is not at fault. If an incense-shop back office writes "alley-mouth proxy burn" as a release, the fine returns to the shop sign, not to the night-shift person.

This page explains location only. What the deceased is called, and which table they enter, is not a field on a notice board.

The tap in the old grain depot rear yard froze and cracked once last year. The street office swapped in a plastic valve. Staff recognize the valve. They do not recognize an incense-shop receipt. Whoever writes the alley empty patch as a "designated site" gets the shop name on the citation.

The red line is spray paint, not chalk. Rain smears it. Smeared is still banned. Residents who argue from a smeared line still meet staff who recognize the valve.
