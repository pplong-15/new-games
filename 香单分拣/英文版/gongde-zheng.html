Neither Ji Shoucheng nor Ji Boyuan is in this column. If Boyuan is to go on the ancestral-seat donor list, the family must register in daylight. A night agency web-order cannot do that step.

If someone copies an unclaimed-seat name into an ancestral-seat application, this temple's clerk will only answer "please change column." The right to change columns is not at the incense shop either.

The Lan-house row has no link to the Ji house. It is here so agency staff do not read "Lan" as "Ji." Shang Deng said it once: look at the surname, not at adjacent lamp numbers.
