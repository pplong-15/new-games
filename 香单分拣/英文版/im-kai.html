13th, 16:10 Mi Sui hand

Caller said he was Ji Kaiping, Boyuan's grandson. He said the tablet is still the grandfather, death anniversary June 19. Dongguan younger female cousin (biaomei) Ji Wanqiu will fill the web form wild and write Shoucheng as late father.

Kaiping repeated June 19 twice. He said Shoucheng is on Wanqiu's mother's side, died away from home, no children, association clothing every year. He does not agree to niche entry. He will not come to the shop. He wants night shift to watch it.

An extract is not a recording. I cannot write the tone in the receiver in full. This sheet can only show there was such a call that afternoon. It does not prove a shrine hall already met.

Mi Sui added: do not walk it blind. Then she went to make change.

Kaiping's own number she wrote at the book edge: reachable in daylight. Night shift does not call back. A call-back becomes a walk-in. This shift does not see walk-ins.

At the end she scribbled out half a curse. What you can still see is the death anniversary and "cousin filling it wild." Do not pad the rest into minutes of a meeting.
