A page for night shift

Ancestral seat: shop ledger, death anniversary, applicant can match the house branch — only then push toward the ancestral-niche table. Solitary-soul table: no heir, died away from home, listed by the association — open separately. Do not enter another family's niche.

Lamp offering follows Ullambana merit at the temple. Lamp number matches the donor name and that is enough. Do not write it as niche entry on the side. River lamps follow city-management sites. Wrong site: hold.

Last year someone filled an unclaimed name into ancestral seat. Print sheets voided all morning. Huo Mai took the shouting. The rule is still these three sentences. Do not invent a fourth table.

This page cannot explain how a rite is done. It also does not teach how any gate is opened. People who ask for a formula can come in daylight. Daylight does not sell a formula either.

River lamps are not a fourth table. Site matches: log them with lamp offering. Site fails: do not take the job. Do not give river lamps another name. Print will tangle.

Last year Huo Mai moved Shoucheng off ancestral seat by this page. The fine slip is in the iron cabinet. The log is on the site she stopped. The two match, so it is not a story she made later.
