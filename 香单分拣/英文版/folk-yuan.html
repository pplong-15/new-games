Zhongyuan and Ullambana (archive entry)

No.: Folk-15Source: seasonal card

The card writes household ancestor rites, Daoist Zhongyuan, and Buddhist Ullambana in the same week. The verbs are roster, release lamps, split tables. Not opening a gate to receive ghosts.

The Mulian story is kept here only as a template of filial debt. It is not written as a required descent into hell. Present-day entries are online lamp offerings, city-management burn bans, hometown-association notices. If a tourism piece writes ghost month as a how-to, this archive does not take it.

The entry has no Ji Shoucheng and no Ji Boyuan. To match those two people tonight, go to the incense-shop ledger and the association roster. Do not look for private names in the holdings.

The 14th or the 15th — Meiwan streets run both. This card does not decide for the incense shop which day to print tonight. After the window closes, August cannot be back-dated as "done on time" — that sentence is the archive talking to festival files, not to this shift's web-orders.
