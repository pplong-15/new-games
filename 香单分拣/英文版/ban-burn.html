July temporary notice Nanmen Alley, embankment stretches

From lunar July 14 through 16, burning paper clothing and paper horses is prohibited on sidewalks, planting strips, and residential stair mouths along Nanmen Alley and the east embankment. Persons in violation shall be asked to leave under the city-appearance rules. Those who refuse will have the shop sign cited.

The designated burn site is the rear yard of the old grain depot, staffed by the street office. Incense shops and paper-craft shops shall not issue slips "permitting alley-mouth burning." This notice does not examine the identity of the deceased. It examines location only.
