A lamp offering is not niche entry

Lamps hang in the corridor. Tablets stay at home. Do not write two things as one. Money paid online only guarantees lamp oil. It does not guarantee one more character on an ancestral niche.

During Ullambana this temple opens a separate unclaimed-seat column for names listed by hometown associations and street offices. A person in that column does not move to ancestral seat because someone called them "late father."

Shang Deng's duty note: if a night incense shop reprints a lamp ticket as ancestral seat, at morning bell I will pull that lamp number off the agency channel and leave only the window original.

This notice does not teach how a rite is performed. It does not teach how any gate is opened. People who ask for a formula, please go back.

Lamp oil is counted by night. A lapsed lamp number is struck from the window blackboard. It does not become an unclaimed seat by itself, and it does not become ancestral seat. Tong Shuang's Lamp-0881 is still there. Do not add her a niche on the side.
