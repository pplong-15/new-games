Died-away roster

July business

Night of the 14th. Unclaimed-dead clothing still follows the roster. Private ancestral niches are not changed.

This association is an invented hometown body. For those who died away from home with no heir, the association lists one clothing-offering table each year. Cost walks association dues, not a house's incense fire.

Clerk Situ Qiu duty note: a private web-order writing a roster name as late father has no link to association business. The association does not recognize that re-hang, and it does not authorize an incense shop to change it.

The roster is not a genealogy. A listed name only shows the association once paid for clothing. It does not show the dead wished to enter anyone's niche, and still less that living kin agreed.

The office sits across from the old grain depot. People in daylight. Door locked at night. The page stays for agency checking. Visitors should read the notice first. Do not treat this page as an emergency window.

The dues column is paused tonight. Clicking it will not issue a payment slip, and it will not take Shoucheng off the roster.

Office hours are in the footer. Night leaves the webpage only. The outbound mailbox is opened in daylight. Visitors please bring a membership number. Do not bring incense.
