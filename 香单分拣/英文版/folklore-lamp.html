That year I stood on the west stretch

Meiwan Night Read2024-08-18Notes

West water-access platform was people on people. East was dark. Someone said quiet. Staff pushed us west. Some lamp shells later drifted to the rail. Sanitation fished them with a net.

Somebody treats a lamp as a letter to one person. I only saw paper and a candle. Do not copy this into an agency note and call it a release permit. I later saw a screenshot in an incense-shop group with the caption "Night Read said you can release." I did not say that.

The east later got a red line. The red line is not feng shui. It is the intake. Anyone who wants to release under the second tree east of the bridge: the city-management page already wrote banned.

That day I did not write anyone's dead. If a name is ballpointed on a lamp, staff make you rub it off before release. That name is not in this piece. Readers should not ask me to fill one in either. The west lamps that night looked good. Looking good does not stamp a chop.
