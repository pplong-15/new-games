Handwritten fine slip Qu Nan's pen

Ji-house ancestral seat: Ji Boyuan. Death anniversary June 19. Entered in 2008. Grandson Ji Kaiping is still in Meiwan. Phone can be reached in daylight.

One remark written dead: Shoucheng is not entered in the niche. Huo Mai changed the table once in July 2025. Print sheets voided. If anyone fills Shoucheng into ancestral seat again, hold first.

July 12 is not this house's day. That is the association's day for burning unclaimed-dead clothing for Shoucheng. Huo Mai marked it in pencil at the edge, then traced it again in ballpoint so night shift would not miss it.

The fine slip only binds this shop's table set. If Kaiping wants the characters on the niche changed, he comes in daylight. Night shift does not carve for him.

Iron-cabinet keys: one each for Qu Nan and Mi Sui. The key you have tonight is the drawer cabinet key. You may read the fine slip. You may not pull it. Pull it and morning reconcile is short one page.

Kaiping's number is in the internal book, not on this ledger slip. The ledger governs table set. It does not govern who comes to shout.
