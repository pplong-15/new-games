Mai, Stopped

after I quit I don't write shop stuff

that night last year earlier

the morning of the 15th when I got yelled at

Posted 2025-08-31 02:17 | Category: night shift | Comments (0)

somebody wanted Ji Shoucheng moved to ancestral seat. ledger said Boyuan, June 19. I changed it to solitary-soul table. print sheets voided. Qu Nan said it should be changed. Mi Sui said the customer would shout. both right. I got the yelling.

I had seen Shoucheng's name on the hometown-association roster. no heir. who wants him stuffed into the Ji niche I don't know. I only know if I released it the morning table card would be a joke.

this is for me. if the shop still has the iron cabinet the fine slip should still be there. don't ask me for a formula. I don't have one. I only change tables.

Qu Nan said later it should be changed. Mi Sui said later the customer would shout. I heard both. if I sat that night again I would still change it. then wait to get yelled at.
