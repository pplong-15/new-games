Meiwan City Management Notice

River temporary column

Alley notice Alley site

East embankment no release, 14th through 16th

From the second tree east of the bridge to the sewage lift well, river lamps are prohibited at night. The west water-access platform remains open. A named ticket is required. Residual fire must be put out on site.

Someone drew the east stretch into a sketch because it looks quiet. Last year lamp shells from that quiet stretch drifted to the intake. Sanitation worked overtime. This column does not discuss whether a lamp is for a particular person. It only marks which stretch cannot release.

The Night Read piece said river lamps look good. Looking good is her copy. This column does not recognize copy.

West tickets need an ID card. The east has no tickets to issue. If an agency treats an east sketch as the west, and lamps drift to the lift well, sanitation will look for the shop. Not for Night Read.
