The spoken-slip line

Route slip, hand-copied into the yellow column

The north-slope side gate is a cut lower than the south-steps gate. A goods truck can enter. The union coach cannot. Pi Houshan knows the side gate. He does not know the south-steps booth. Four rows on the slip he pressed through carbon paper. Writing leans right. On the way back he took a shortcut and still did not loop the memorial. Someone asked if he saw ash. He said box down, leave. The ditch is not delivery's.
