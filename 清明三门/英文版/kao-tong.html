Please pass on to comrades still not at the south steps

From: Miao Shiqiao · grain-depot union

All: Qingming day, south-steps sign-in short. Collective code already issued. Do not change yourselves to another place. Attendance boxes empty and I cannot file my side.

A family member said they would go to the north slope first. Please manage that yourselves. Union vehicles run the south steps once only.

If the reservation desk receives a number-change request, please do not process it. We only want boxes.

Blank sheet　The other letter

Union boilerplate · headcount added at the end

The chase letter opens with "All" because it is a blast. The middle line — do not change yourselves to another place — is written at people like Gu Wanhe. No name pointed. Vehicles run the south steps once only: the union motor pool decided that, not the garden. Reservation desk, do not process a number change — he copied that to Hang Shu and to the daytime window. Headcount: see the blank sheet. He will not list it again in the letter.
