What these sources cannot prove

Pinned on the cabinet sideTiao Wan had it copied

The complaint can prove ash on the neighbor line. A person coming out of a grave is not established by these sources.

The household card can prove a private-grave plot. Family intent is not established by these sources.

The batch can prove the code was issued to the union for the south steps. The public pages cannot determine that every code-holder stood there.

The receipt can prove goods were put down at grave 37. It cannot determine whether the south steps were empty.

If the ledger shows one code, two records, it can prove the booking was written twice. Who authorized it is not established by these sources.

The lore thread can prove people like to tell it. This specific connection is ruled out as a closing nature.

The old ticket can prove ash last year. It cannot name tonight.

Attendance blanks can prove people were wanted that day. They are not a reason to change a number.
