Arrive

  1. Gather at the south steps on Qingming day.
  2. Hold the collective code to enter the gate.
  3. Sign-in counts as a unit box.
  4. Flowers are placed on the south steps by the garden. Not delivered to the north slope.

Do not write

  1. Do not write a personal private-grave number.
  2. Do not teach a field paper-burn.
  3. Paper-money fire control belongs to garden patrol.
  4. Missing people: the union marks its own boxes.

The notice can prove how the south steps run. Whether Plot 12 had anyone that day is not established by these sources.

South-steps explainer-board copy

The south-steps explainer board has been rained on. The copy traces the blistered characters in black. Arrive, hold the code, sign in, flowers placed together — four lines that can walk. Personal private grave, field paper-burn, paper-money fire control, missing-person boxes — four lines not written for visitors. Written for night shift. The notice proves how the south steps run. It cannot prove anyone was waiting on the north slope for a car.
