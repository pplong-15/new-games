Public notice on host of record for Gu Houshan touqi

Gu Houshan of Dongbu Group 1 died of illness on August 15. At 15:00 today, at the temporary tables, we asked who could answer as host. The person present who could answer was second branch, Gu Zhonghuai. Accordingly mimeographed: touqi host of record is second branch, Gu Zhonghuai.

Luan Shouyi notes: eldest son Gu Bochuan was not in the village at that time. Married-out daughter Gu Jiping’s coach had not arrived. Once the mimeograph warmed, the sentence was set. If there is a correction at night, it goes as a radio-station recommendation. This board will not scrape characters on the floor.

Host of record is not gift-register principal. Principal is Sheng Mai’s copy. This board only settles who was answering this afternoon.

Printed paper is hard to change on the floor. At the 22:40 reading this sentence was read as well, so nobody thinks the village can still wipe it by hand.

A purple stripe on the mimeograph edge is a dirty roller. Dirty is not an erratum. An erratum would be another sheet. No time tonight.

Sheng Mai was also present and answering at 15:00. Sheng Mai answers for the book, not for host of record, so he was not written.

Mimeograph file copy in the clerk drawer　23/42
