Dongbu village board

Day-count slip　posted August 18

Home Host West wing

Gu house seven-day start and stop

Death date August 15. Touqi runs from the night of August 20 through the morning of August 21. Do not crush the sevens into one daylight.

Second seven-day through sixth seven-day are on another table. Clerk room will post when it has time. If a married-out daughter takes the sixth seven-day, do not rewrite touqi on this slip.

Someone hears “sixth night” as a door that must be opened for a soul. This board only records the offering table and the slot. It does not write blades, footprints, or driving-out. Do not put those words into broadcast copy.

If radio night shift matches this slip to the axis, 22:00 to 23:30 on the axis is the touqi main stretch. A person on the last coach is late. Not a punishment for missing the main stretch. Not present on the main stretch either.

Who takes the offering table down after the morning of August 21 is on another unposted draft. Unposted is not a public notice. Night shift does not handle it. The thumbtack is rusted. A corner of the slip has lifted. Rain will seep in at the lift.
