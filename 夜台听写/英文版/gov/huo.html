Dongbu village board

Corridor safety　not a household branch

Home Days Iron box

Do not stack paper funeral goods in the west wing of the temporary tables

Old-house west-wing door is narrow. Paper goods and wire in the corridor, one cigarette end is enough. Spoken on the 19th. This paper now.

The west wing may hold Sheng Mai’s iron box. Box is low. It does not block the door. Paper figures, gold mountains, paper televisions: move them onto the brick yard, away from the wires.

West wing in this slip is a room. Someone hears “money’s in the west room” on a transcript and thinks a fourth branch grew on the genealogy. That is hearing a direction as a share. The clerk room does not recognize a fourth branch.

Basins and brooms that overflowed from Huaiji: do not stuff them back into the west wing tonight either. Zhonghuai nodded to that himself.

I measured the corridor with my feet. Two and a half. If a paper-goods base is wider, the door will not shut. Door that will not shut, smoke goes into the house.

This slip is for fire　not for gift-money name-share　25/42
