Dongbu Group 1, Gu Houshan dies of illness

Correspondent copy　death date August 15

Gu Houshan, 72, Dongbu Group 1. Died of illness August 15 at the town clinic. Not on the accident list. Wife deceased. Sons Gu Bochuan, Gu Zhonghuai; daughter Gu Jiping (married into Liupu).

Touqi runs from the sixth night through the morning of the seventh day, at temporary tables in the old house. No open viewing. No out-of-town reporters in the hall.

The copy only names three children. It does not name a host of record. Host of record is a later village mimeograph. The evening paper’s archive did not revise this notice.

No death face printed. Readers who hear “a father’s voice” elsewhere should first ask whether it is a stored night-slot tape from an old year. Do not write it as a returned-soul sighting.

Clinic visiting hours and standing water on the tractor road ran the same day as this notice. They have been pulled to the side column. Pulled, they still hold a number, so nobody says the archive is empty.

“Married into Liupu” is old wording. Old wording does not mean she pays touqi tonight.
