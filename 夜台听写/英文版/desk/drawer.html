1. Names that come up in the transcript: match them to the book and the night board. If they do not match, leave them blank. Blank is cheaper than wrong.

2. If a proxy mark and a clock-in show up the same night. Person and money do not have to live in the same mouth. Wrote this twice. Second time changed “must” to “do not have to.”

3. Hear the ending of 22:14 first. Then go to classifieds and see how Huaiji finishes a line. The woman’s cut against the elder-branch entry. West room has two meanings. One is on the fire page. One is the iron box. Do not mash them.

4. On the main stretch you can match Zhonghuai, Wanqiu, Houshui, Luan Shouyi. The clip that claims Bochuan ends the same way the shop does. The clip that brings money, against the proxy mark, is Wanqiu. First-seven money: elder branch, paid by proxy. Bochuan is in the county. Jiping only hits the last coach. Do not tick either on the main stretch. This slip is full. Did not want it this full. If you already matched the papers, treat me as talking too much.

Slips are the back of scrap. Front is a daytime water-cut notice, void. Do not treat the water-cut face as another transcript. Drawer rail sticks. When it sticks, hear the axis first, then lift a slip.
