A proxy mark is not a shame. It is person not here, money here. Host of record is printed on the village board, not in this book. When two papers fight, which one stands is for the radio station to mark.

Carbon fades when you lift it. The proxy on the right of page A still reads. Three lines on page B I touched up once in pencil, afraid of bleed.
