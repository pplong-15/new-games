Page B　second branch

Oddsafternoon into night, in pieces

Account name Gu Zhonghuai. Three entries: soy sauce, the difference on extra dishes, one bundle of thin wire for paper frames. No touqi principal.

Zhonghuai said it himself: principal stays with the elder branch, he only runs the floor. A person who runs the floor can be written host of record. That is the village mimeograph. This book does not recognize those two words.

The wire slip can also be seen in the Huaiji classified. People who want money from him want the person who can answer on the floor, not a principal on this page.

If you write first-seven money to the second branch, you lift incidentals into principal. Neighbors who open the book later will say I kept it muddled. I write that on the copy’s edge first.

Soy sauce is morning. Extra dishes after noon. Wire near dusk. Three times, pulled apart. Not one principal line.

Zhonghuai told me to bind the incidentals. Said do not set them with the elder-branch envelope, or someone in the morning will say the second branch paid principal too.
