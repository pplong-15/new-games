Page A　elder branch

Receivedinto the box at 22:18

Account name Gu Bochuan. Himself did not sign at the book. Payer written as Tang Wanqiu, a proxy mark on the right. Amount follows the spoken price of touqi tables and paper goods. I only record what entered the box. I do not audit the price.

Wanqiu set the envelope on the table. Said he is on nights, she would pay by proxy, and she would leave when it was down. I asked if we should wait for his signature. She shook her head. County still has a night to sit.

This line counts as elder branch paying first-seven money. Person not here. Name-share still on the elder branch. If the broadcast also writes Bochuan present, it will not match this page.

On the 22:22 transcript someone told me to write proxy. This square.

Envelope is kraft. Corner has the tooth-holes of a county-hospital registration slip, like it was torn off a triage desk for a sleeve. Cash inside. I counted. I do not write the number on the copy, or someone will take it for a going rate.

Wanqiu left without looking back at the tables. The one who looks back at the tables is Zhonghuai. He is waiting on the broadcast.
