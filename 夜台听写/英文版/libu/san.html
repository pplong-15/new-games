Page C　Gu Jiping

Heldleft empty while she is not here

This afternoon I drew the grid first. Married-out daughters often carry the sixth seven-day. Touqi principal is not written onto her. She sent word: fare is her own, the sixth-seven packet is wrapped separate, leave her out of tonight.

Before 23:40 this square is empty. Empty is not a miss. She is still on the road. Reading her name into the hall is not an envelope into the box.

If she enters the hall she only adds a sixth-seven intention. I start another line. I do not change page A. Page A already has a proxy mark. Nothing to do with her.

If the coach line still has her slip, match “sixth seven wrapped separate” to the edge of this page. That is enough.

I still remember her childhood name. I do not write it in the grid. The grid only knows Jiping. A childhood name on a transcript is not presence either. Only old people in the house still use it. Broadcast copy does not.

held
