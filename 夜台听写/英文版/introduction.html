Shuangqiao Radio — Night Shift Sheet

Who takes this shift

Staff ID: Pu Sheng. This sheet was printed this afternoon and left in the changing-room locker. Zhai Taisheng signed the line under it. Tonight is touqi, the first seven-day memorial, for Gu Houshan of Dongbu. From 20:00 the tuner stacks the cheap FM head in the hall, the village reading, and an empty frequency that has sat quiet for years onto one axis.

You are not the host. You click the slots on that axis, set them against the books and notices saved on this machine, and mark three items: who is on the floor, which line came from which mouth, and which household branch the first-seven money is written to.

What you can do

Authority stops at a recommendation. Get the form into station mail before 05:30. You do not read it over the loudspeaker. You do not copy the original audio out. You do not slip over to the house where the wake is laid. Ji Lengshan watches the machine in the hall.

Names will come up in the transcript. Treat a name as sound first, not as presence. Mi Qiukui left the day book on the left of the keyboard. She wrote: finish the slots first. The drawer has slips she tore off. If you stick, open those.

Take the shift. Open the duty machine

Notes on the paper

Shuangqiao, Dongbu, and the names on this sheet are station names. Ask them outside town and you will not get a match. Whether the 06:00 town broadcast changes its line depends on the three items you file, not on who you heard crying in the night.

The mixer photo has no station mark. The words on the knobs were already worn flat. After you enter the machine this sheet does not come back. If a name sticks, open the drawer.

It is raining outside. Rain does not enter the axis. The axis takes the FM head and the empty frequency. It does not take the water in your ears.

Shift sheet stays in the changing room. Do not turn it over again after you enter the machine
