Ticket-window clip

Gu Jiping, Liupu last coach. Please have the driver let her phone first at the stop. She wrote the slip herself. Ballpoint. Grease on the edge.

Back still has half a line: sixth seven wrapped separate, do not write me on touqi. Ticket clerk said she repeated that to the next seat as soon as she boarded, afraid someone in Shuangqiao would put a pen down first.

Before 23:40 she is not in Dongbu. If her name comes up on the 22:31 transcript, that is reading bleed, not her standing by the altar table.

Driver will not be in a picture. This page has no cabin photo.

Next seat was an old woman going to Shuangqiao to see family. She later parroted to the clerk: that woman talked sixth seven the whole way, not touqi. Parrot is not evidence. The slip is.

Grease is from a pancake wrapper on the bus. A pancake wrapper is not a charm. The half line on the back is oil-soaked and still readable.
