## Positioning

Working title: **Night Desk Transcript**.

The edition must read as an English-language night shift *inside* a Chinese town radio station. The player solves provenance (which mouth, which paper), identity (self-name ≠ present), chronology (main stretch vs last coach), and rule-meaning (host of record ≠ paying branch; west room ≠ a fourth branch)—not a Chinese literacy test.

This is a timeline-click game (Unheard-style). There is no clip-search box. Eight slots open from the green bars. English-only click-through is the intended path.

## Evidence limits (do not inflate)

- The duty-machine transcript is not a courtroom tape. The hall FM head, village reading, and an old cassette can stack on one timeline. A self-name is a sound. Presence is a paper problem.
- Gu Bochuan’s Ward 3 clock-in at 21:50 and the triage extract (“That’ll do, yeah”) are what the public night board can show. They do not publish a chart or a bed number.
- “He’s back” on 23:05 proves an old grain cassette was played into 87.3. It does **not** establish a returned soul. Use: `not established by these sources`.
- Host of record on the mimeograph is who could answer at 15:00. It is not automatically the touqi paying branch.
- Paid-by-proxy on the elder-branch line means money in the box, person not at the book. It is not presence and not a legal confession.
- “Money’s in the west room” is a location. Village fire copy and the iron-box page write **west wing**, a room. The public pages cannot determine a fourth household branch. Writing west room as a paying branch is a player error path, not a new fact.
- Overlay “married-out daughter, Gu Jiping” at 22:31 is bleed from Luan Shouyi’s draft. The last Liupu coach is 23:40. This specific connection (Jiping at the altar at 22:31) is ruled out by the timetable.
- Night shift files a recommendation. It does not approve who pays, rewrite the gift register, or go to the hall.
- Missing proof is not innocence. Empty checkboxes are a hold, not an exoneration.
