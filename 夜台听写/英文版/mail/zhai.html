Three items by 05:30

From: Zhai Taisheng
To: Pu Sheng
Time: 16:41

Village stuffed the mimeograph over this afternoon. Host of record written as second branch. Later someone in the hall shouted Bochuan. I caught an ear of it.

You mark: who is present, who is speaking, which branch the money is written to. At 06:00 I need one sentence that stands. If there is a correction, say it. If there is not, do not pull the rug.

Recommendation only. Do not claim an account for any branch. I had triage forward a public night roster. Pinned it on the desktop. In town or not: look at the clock-in. Not at who is loud.

I am tired. You file.

No attachment. I will not stuff a mimeograph scan into station mail. Original is on the village board. If you need to match it, open the saved copy. Do not wait for me to forward.

This letter stays on the duty machine　do not copy it onto loudspeaker paper　13/42
