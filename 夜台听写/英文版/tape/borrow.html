Index Grain

August 20　one circle

Borrow book　line 32

Gu Houshui. Around 18:00. The grain-notice box. He cannot write. Burned a circle in the square with a lighter. I added later: younger brother of the deceased. Not a paying branch.

He asked for “elder brother talking.” I explained it is an old intake notice. He nodded. Still stuffed the box into his jacket.

Ji Lengshan said anyone can press play on the hall FM head, if the tape is there. The sand at 23:05 matches this circle.

The three items can be filed without the borrow page. You turn it up only so you do not hear weigh-in as a returned soul.

The lighter burned through a little of the square. Edge is scorched yellow. Scorched yellow is not a charm. Whether he brings the box back by dawn, I wrote on the return line.

Jacket is blue. Pocket is shallow. The box shows a corner in a shallow pocket. He walked with a hand on it.

Return

  • Due before dawn
  • Not back: keep the circle
