1998　summer grain

Index Borrow

Old night-slot dictation　west end of the drying ground

Entered　recopied by Mi Qiukui

Notice to all households. Summer grain intake. Weigh-in at the west end of the drying ground. Damp first — spread it. Do not bag in a hurry. Weigh-in man: Gu Houshan. This night slot is read slow. Someone at home writes.

Copy ends here. No wake. No children’s name-share. No host of record. Jack this cut onto tonight’s axis and you only prove an old cassette was played for a stretch.

Houshui likes to say “he’s back.” What came back is this plastic shell. Whether a soul came back, this index does not write, and cannot write.

The old male layer at 23:05: wording matches drying ground and weigh-in. Numbers were already muddy that year. Tonight they are muddier.

When I recopied I kept “notice to all households,” because those words are night-slot stock. Stock is not a spell. It is a signal to pick up a pen.

Damp first, spread it: August 1998 rained in a row. That rain and tonight’s wake are not the same weather.
