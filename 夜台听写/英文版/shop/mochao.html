Local Sign-off Wire

Huaiji Hardware　pinned

Nails, line, basins, brooms, thin wire. Shutter half down at night. If you want stock, shout first.

Price is on the goods. I will not grind. Sign-off is one line: Don’t haggle the price. Look at the goods.

Neighbors have borrowed the line for a joke. My brother does not end a sentence that way. In the county he likes to drag a “yeah.”

Whoever put “don’t haggle” on a close-mic transcript, that is not a county mouth. Shop people can hear it.

Tonight I am running the floor. Shop is on the apprentice for now. The apprentice will not post a new ad with my sign-off.

Four years ago someone changed the sign-off to “price negotiable.” I changed it back the same day. Price negotiable is not me.

If the apprentice writes “negotiable,” treat it as a slip of the hand. Not a new catchphrase.
